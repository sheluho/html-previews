Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our documented cleaning protocols.

How often should professional carpet cleaning be scheduled?
For typical residential environments, industry standards recommend professional steam cleaning every 12 to 18 months. Homes with high traffic, children, or pets may require more frequent sessions to maintain optimal hygiene and extend the life of the fibers.
Are all stains eligible for complete removal?
While advanced stain removal techniques are highly effective against a wide array of common spills (including wine, coffee, and pet accidents), certain chemical reactions can permanently alter textile dyes. Technicians assess each spot individually to determine the safest and most effective extraction method.
What is the expected drying time after steam cleaning?
Modern extraction equipment removes the vast majority of moisture during the cleaning process. Generally, carpets and upholstery will be dry to the touch within 4 to 8 hours, depending on ambient humidity, temperature, and the specific density of the fabric treated.
Is your upholstery cleaning safe for delicate fabrics?
Yes. Technicians conduct a thorough pre-inspection to identify fabric composition and structural integrity. Cleaning protocols are then adjusted—utilizing specific temperatures and specialized solutions—to ensure the safe and effective restoration of delicate upholstery.
How does rug cleaning differ from standard carpet care?
Area and oriental rugs often feature intricate weaves, natural dyes, and sensitive fibers (like wool or silk) that require bespoke handling. Rug cleaning utilizes distinct, gentler washing and drying processes compared to the high-pressure extraction typically used for wall-to-wall synthetic carpeting.
