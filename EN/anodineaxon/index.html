Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a turnkey kitchen remodeling project?

A turnkey kitchen project includes end-to-end service. This generally covers everything from the initial design consultation and demolition to plumbing, electrical, custom cabinetry installation, countertops, and the final coat of paint. You simply walk into your completed space.

How long does a custom kitchen renovation typically take?

Timelines vary depending on the scope of the project. A full-scale custom kitchen remodel can take anywhere from 6 to 12 weeks once construction begins. Material availability, especially for specialized cabinets and imported countertops, can also impact the schedule.

Can I choose my own cabinets and countertops?

Absolutely. The core of a custom kitchen is personalization. You will collaborate with design experts to select the exact materials, finishes, and hardware that match your functional needs and aesthetic preferences.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical work during the remodel?

Yes, through our network of licensed service providers. Kitchen remodeling often requires significant adjustments to plumbing and electrical systems to accommodate new appliances and layouts. All structural and utility work is managed by qualified professionals.

How do I prepare my home for a major kitchen remodel?

Preparation involves clearing out all personal items from your existing kitchen cabinets and counters. You should also set up a temporary kitchen area in another part of your house with essentials like a microwave and refrigerator, as you will not have access to the main kitchen during active construction.
