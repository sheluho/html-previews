Common Questions

What does a turnkey renovation include?

A turnkey renovation means the entire process is handled for you by the general contractor. This includes initial architectural design, acquiring necessary building permits, sourcing all materials, managing subcontractors, and executing the full construction phase. You simply turn the key and walk into your completed home.

How long does a whole home remodeling project take?

Timelines vary significantly based on the scope of work. A comprehensive whole home remodeling project can take anywhere from three to eight months of active construction, preceded by several weeks of design and planning. Your general contractor will provide a detailed schedule before any demolition begins.

Do you manage structural home additions?

Yes, managing structural home additions is a core competency of the design-build teams we promote. Whether extending a kitchen outward, adding a second story, or building an attached sunroom, the process ensures the new structure integrates flawlessly with your home's existing foundation and roofline.

Why should I hire a general contractor instead of managing trades myself?

Acting as your own project manager is incredibly time-consuming and risky without industry experience. A professional general contractor handles scheduling, quality control, liability insurance, and building code compliance. They ensure tradesmen arrive on time, in the right order, and perform work to the highest standards.

Can a home renovation improve my home's accessibility?

Absolutely. Many clients choose a home renovation to create barrier-free, comfortable living spaces that adapt to their needs over time. This includes widening doorways, installing zero-threshold showers, and optimizing kitchen layouts for ease of use without sacrificing high-end design aesthetics.
