Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the wedding planning process.

What is the difference between full-service wedding planning and a day of coordinator?

A full-service wedding planner is involved from the very beginning, handling design, budget, and vendor sourcing. A day of coordinator steps in closer to the wedding date to manage logistics and ensure the plans you've already made run smoothly.

Do you assist with destination wedding arrangements?

Yes, absolutely. Organizing a destination wedding requires specialized logistical knowledge. The vendors we promote can coordinate travel, remote venues, and local suppliers to make your faraway celebration seamless.

When should I hire a wedding coordinator?

It is highly recommended to secure your wedding coordinator at least 9 to 12 months in advance. Popular dates fill up quickly, and early booking ensures you have expert guidance throughout your planning journey.

Can a wedding planner help us stay within our budget?

Yes. One of the greatest values of a full-service wedding planning company is their ability to maximize your budget. They have established vendor relationships and know how to allocate funds effectively to achieve your vision.

Will the day of coordinator handle setup and breakdown?

A day of coordinator primarily focuses on managing the timeline, directing vendors, and resolving issues. While they oversee the setup to ensure it matches your design, heavy lifting and primary setup/breakdown are usually handled by the specific rental or venue staff.
