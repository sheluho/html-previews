Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a windshield replacement take?
A standard windshield replacement typically takes about one to two hours to complete. After installation, there is usually a mandatory safe drive-away time of about one hour to allow the urethane adhesive to cure properly.
Can my windshield be repaired instead of replaced?
Generally, if a rock chip is smaller than a quarter and is not located directly in the driver's line of sight, a simple chip repair is possible. Larger cracks or damage near the edges usually require a full windshield replacement.
Is auto glass replacement covered by insurance?
Most comprehensive auto insurance policies cover auto glass repair and replacement. Depending on your specific policy and deductible, windshield repair might even be covered with zero out-of-pocket cost to you.
Do you handle side car window replacements?
Yes, the service providers we promote handle all types of auto glass, including side door windows, rear windshields, and quarter glass. Car window replacement is performed using high-quality glass to ensure proper fit and function.
How soon can I wash my car after new auto glass is installed?
It is highly recommended to wait at least 24 to 48 hours before taking your vehicle through a high-pressure car wash after a windshield replacement. This gives the urethane seal adequate time to cure completely.
