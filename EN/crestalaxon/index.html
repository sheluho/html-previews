Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book our wedding photographer?
We recommend securing your date 12 to 18 months in advance, especially for peak season dates. Our calendar fills quickly, and early booking ensures we are available to document your celebration.
Are engagement photos included in your collections?
Yes, many of our comprehensive collections include a complimentary session for engagement photos. We find this is a wonderful way for us to connect before the wedding day, allowing you to feel completely comfortable in front of our lenses.
What is your approach to bridal portraits?
We approach bridal portraits with an editorial mindset. Whether scheduled on a separate day or carved out during your timeline, we focus on utilizing natural light, architectural elements, and your personal style to create striking, timeless imagery.
Do you travel for destination wedding photography?
Absolutely. We are passionate about destination wedding photography and are available to travel worldwide. We handle our own travel arrangements and provide custom quotes based on the location and scope of your multi-day event.
How long does it take to receive our final gallery?
We typically deliver a curated preview within one week of your event. The complete, meticulously edited wedding photography gallery is delivered within 8 to 10 weeks, ensuring each image meets our highest standards of quality.
