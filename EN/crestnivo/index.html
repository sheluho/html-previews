Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about tire care and seasonal storage.

Why do I need professional tire storage?

Tires take up a significant amount of space and can be incredibly heavy and dirty to move. Professional tire storage keeps them in a secure, climate-monitored environment, preventing rubber degradation from extreme temperatures while keeping your home clutter-free.

How often should I get a tire rotation?

It is generally recommended to have a tire rotation every 5,000 to 7,000 miles, or whenever you get an oil change. Regular rotation ensures even wear across all tires, which maximizes their lifespan and maintains optimal handling.

What is included in a standard tire installation?

A complete tire installation includes safely dismounting the old tires, mounting the new ones onto your wheels, precision electronic balancing to prevent vibrations, installing new valve stems if necessary, and torquing the lug nuts to the manufacturer's exact specifications.

When is a complete tire replacement necessary?

Tire replacement is crucial when your tread depth falls below 2/32 of an inch, if you notice significant uneven wear, deep cracks in the sidewall, or bulges. Driving on compromised tires drastically reduces braking efficiency and increases the risk of blowouts.

Does wheel alignment actually affect tire wear?

Absolutely. If your vehicle requires a wheel alignment, the tires are not hitting the road at the correct angle. This causes rapid, uneven wear on the edges of the tread. Proper alignment protects your investment in new tires and improves steering response.
