Frequently Asked Questions

What does your standard lawn care service include?

Standard lawn care typically includes routine lawn mowing, edging along walkways and driveways, trimming around obstacles, and blowing debris off hard surfaces to keep your property looking pristine.

Do you offer full landscape installation?

Yes, our partners specialize in complete landscape installation. This includes tearing out old landscaping, comprehensive garden design, soil preparation, planting, and installing mulch or decorative stone.

Is tree service part of your offerings?

Absolutely. Professional tree service, including structural pruning, shaping, deadwood removal, and overall tree health assessments, is a core part of the comprehensive landscaping services highlighted here.

How often should I schedule lawn mowing?

For optimal turf health during the growing season, weekly lawn mowing is highly recommended. Bi-weekly schedules can be arranged during slower growth periods or depending on your specific grass type.

Can you help with custom garden design?

Yes, from the initial concept to the final planting, we connect you with experts who can create a custom garden design tailored to your property's soil, sunlight conditions, and your personal aesthetic preferences.
