Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about residential solar panel installation.

How long does residential solar panel installation typically take?
Once the permits are approved and the custom design is finalized, the actual physical installation of solar panels on your roof usually takes just one to three days, depending on the system's size and complexity.
Will installing solar panels completely eliminate my electric bill?
While a properly sized photovoltaic system can dramatically reduce your monthly energy costs, you may still receive a small bill for grid connection fees or if your energy consumption exceeds your solar power production during certain months.
How do photovoltaic systems perform on cloudy or rainy days?
Solar panels continue to generate electricity on cloudy days, though at a reduced capacity compared to direct sunlight. Ultraviolet rays still penetrate clouds, allowing your residential solar setup to produce measurable power year-round.
What kind of maintenance does a residential solar power system require?
Solar installations are incredibly low maintenance because they have no moving parts. Generally, occasional cleaning to remove dust, leaves, or snow, along with an annual professional inspection, is all that is needed to ensure optimal performance.
Are there financial incentives for switching to solar energy?
Yes, many homeowners can take advantage of federal tax credits, state-level rebates, and local utility incentives when they install residential solar. We recommend consulting with a tax professional to understand exactly which benefits apply to your specific situation.
