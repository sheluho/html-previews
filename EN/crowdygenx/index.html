Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning custom furniture.

What is the typical timeline for a custom dining table?

Crafting a custom dining table requires precise attention to detail. Depending on the complexity of the design, wood availability, and the artisan's schedule, you can typically expect a timeline of 8 to 12 weeks from the final design approval to delivery.

Can I select the specific materials for my custom sofa?

Absolutely. A true custom sofa allows you to choose everything from the hardwood used for the frame to the density of the seat cushions and the final upholstery fabric. The showroom offers an extensive library of premium fabrics and leathers to match your aesthetic.

Do you design bespoke furniture for unique architectural spaces?

Yes, creating bespoke furniture for challenging or unconventional spaces is a specialty of the makers we promote. Whether it is built-in cabinetry for a slanted ceiling or a custom shelving unit for an alcove, skilled artisans will measure and construct pieces that integrate seamlessly into your home.

How does the design consultation process work?

The process begins with a collaborative discussion about your vision, spatial requirements, and stylistic preferences. From there, designers generate sketches and propose material samples. Production only begins once you have reviewed and approved the final specifications for your handmade furniture.

Is handmade furniture a good investment?

Handmade furniture is widely regarded as a superior long-term investment. Unlike mass-produced items that degrade quickly, bespoke pieces are constructed using traditional joinery and solid materials, ensuring they withstand daily use and often appreciate in sentimental and physical value over generations.
