Frequently Asked Questions

What is a turnkey kitchen remodel? ▼

A turnkey kitchen project means that every aspect of your kitchen remodeling is managed under one umbrella. From the initial design and ordering of custom cabinets to the final installation of countertops and hardware, the entire process is streamlined so you don't have to hire multiple independent contractors.

How long does a custom kitchen renovation take? ▼

The timeline varies depending on the complexity of the project and material availability. On average, once the design is finalized and custom materials (like bespoke cabinets) are ordered, the active demolition and installation phase can take anywhere from 3 to 8 weeks.

What materials are used for your custom cabinets? ▼

The cabinets are crafted using premium, durable materials selected for both longevity and aesthetics. Options typically include solid hardwoods for doors and frames, high-grade plywood for cabinet boxes, and robust hardware to ensure smooth operation for years to come.

Can I choose my own countertops? ▼

Absolutely. Countertops are a defining feature of any kitchen. You will have a wide selection of materials to choose from, including natural granite, luxurious marble, and highly durable engineered quartz, ensuring a perfect match for your design vision.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical changes? ▼

Yes, as part of a full-service kitchen remodeling approach, all necessary structural, plumbing, and electrical modifications required to accommodate your new layout are coordinated to ensure the space is fully functional and up to modern standards.
