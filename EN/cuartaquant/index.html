Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom dining table?

Because every piece of bespoke furniture is made to order, timelines vary based on complexity and material availability. Typically, a custom dining table requires 8 to 12 weeks from the finalization of the design to delivery.

Can I provide my own design for a custom sofa?

Absolutely. We frequently collaborate with interior designers and clients who have specific visions. Bring us your sketches or inspiration photos, and our team will engineer the frame and upholstery to make your custom sofa a reality.

What types of wood do you use for handmade furniture?

We source a wide variety of premium, sustainable hardwoods including Walnut, White Oak, Maple, and Cherry. During your consultation, we will discuss the best wood species to match your aesthetic goals and durability needs.

Do you have a showroom where I can see your work?

Yes, as a dedicated custom furniture maker and showroom, we invite you to visit our location to experience the quality of our craftsmanship firsthand, view wood samples, and test the comfort of our seating arrangements.

How do I care for my new bespoke furniture?

We provide detailed care instructions with every piece. Generally, solid wood furniture should be kept away from direct heat sources and wiped with a damp, soft cloth. We recommend specific natural waxes or oils depending on the finish applied to your item.
