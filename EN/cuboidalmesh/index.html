Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

It is generally recommended to have your heating and cooling systems inspected and serviced at least once a year. Ideally, you should check your air conditioner in the spring and your furnace in the fall to ensure they are ready for peak seasons.

What are the signs I need a new AC installation?

If your current unit is over 10-15 years old, requires frequent costly repairs, fails to cool your home evenly, or causes unexplained spikes in your energy bills, it may be time to consult with an HVAC professional about a full replacement.

Is a heat pump better than a traditional furnace?

A heat pump is highly efficient because it transfers heat rather than generating it. It works as both a heater and an air conditioner. However, in extremely cold climates, a traditional furnace might still be necessary as a primary or backup heat source. The best choice depends on your specific regional climate and home infrastructure.

How can I improve my home's heating and cooling efficiency?

Regular filter replacements, sealing ductwork leaks, upgrading to a programmable thermostat, and ensuring your home is properly insulated are fundamental steps. Scheduling routine professional maintenance is also crucial for keeping the equipment running efficiently.
