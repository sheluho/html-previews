Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a wedding planner and a day of coordinator?

A full-service wedding planner is involved from the very beginning, helping you conceptualize the design, set the budget, and hire all vendors. A day of coordinator typically steps in 4 to 6 weeks before the wedding to take over the logistics and execute the plans you have already put in place.

Do you coordinate destination weddings?

Yes, our partners specialize in destination wedding planning. They manage travel accommodations, liaise with international vendors, and ensure that planning a wedding from afar is a smooth and enjoyable process.

When should we book our wedding coordinator?

For full-service wedding planning, we recommend booking 12 to 18 months in advance. If you are looking solely for a day of coordinator, securing your date 6 to 9 months prior ensures availability during busy wedding seasons.

Do you help with event design and styling?

Absolutely. A core part of the comprehensive wedding planning service is establishing a cohesive design concept. Planners work closely with florists, rental companies, and lighting experts to bring your visual aesthetic to life.

Will the coordinator be there for the rehearsal?

Yes. Whether you book full-service planning or a day of coordinator package, managing the wedding rehearsal is typically included to ensure the entire wedding party knows exactly what to do on the big day.
