Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a standard house cleaning?

Our standard house cleaning covers the essential maintenance of your home. This includes dusting surfaces, wiping down kitchen counters and appliance exteriors, thoroughly cleaning bathrooms (toilets, showers, sinks), vacuuming carpets, and mopping hard floors. It is designed to keep your home looking fresh on a regular basis.

Do I need to be home during the maid service?

No, you do not need to be home. Many of our clients prefer to provide a spare key, garage code, or lockbox access so our professionals can perform the maid service while they are at work or running errands. We respect your property and ensure it is securely locked upon our departure.

How does recurring cleaning scheduling work?

We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly recurring cleaning schedules. Once you select a frequency, we reserve a consistent day and time slot for your home. If you need to reschedule a specific visit, we ask for advanced notice so we can adjust our routes accordingly.

What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

While standard cleaning maintains your home's general hygiene, deep cleaning is a more intensive process. Deep cleaning includes hand-wiping baseboards, cleaning the inside of window sills, scrubbing grout lines, and addressing heavy build-up in kitchens and bathrooms. We usually recommend a deep clean for initial visits or seasonal overhauls.

Can I customize my move-out cleaning package?

Absolutely. Move-out cleaning can be tailored to meet the specific requirements of your landlord or real estate agent. You can add on services like interior oven cleaning, interior refrigerator cleaning, or interior cabinet wiping to ensure the property is left in immaculate condition.
