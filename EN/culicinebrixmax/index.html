Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our detailing and protection services.

What is the main benefit of a ceramic coating?

A ceramic coating provides a hard, transparent, and hydrophobic layer over your vehicle's clear coat. This offers superior paint protection against environmental contaminants, bird droppings, UV damage, and chemical stains, while significantly enhancing gloss and making routine washing much easier.

How often should I schedule professional car detailing?

For optimal maintenance, we recommend a comprehensive car detailing service every 4 to 6 months. Regular maintenance washes (every 2-4 weeks) in between these major details will help preserve the protective layers and keep your vehicle looking its best year-round.

Does interior detailing include deep stain removal?

Yes, our thorough interior detailing packages include targeted stain removal for upholstery, carpets, and floor mats. We use specialized extractors and safe chemical cleaners to lift deep-seated dirt and spills, alongside thorough sanitization of all interior surfaces.

What is the difference between traditional waxing and ceramic coating?

Traditional wax provides a temporary shine and minimal protection that typically lasts only a few weeks to a couple of months before wearing off. A ceramic coating bonds with the paint at a molecular level, offering highly durable paint protection that can last for years, resisting heat and harsh chemicals far better than wax.

Can auto detailing fix deep scratches in my paint?

Auto detailing and professional paint correction can significantly reduce or completely eliminate surface-level swirl marks and light scratches that reside within the clear coat. However, scratches that penetrate through the clear coat into the base color or primer cannot be polished out and may require localized paint repair or touch-up.
