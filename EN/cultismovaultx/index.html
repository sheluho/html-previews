Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about upgrading your windows.

What are the main benefits of replacement windows?

New windows provide immediate benefits including significantly improved energy efficiency, enhanced curb appeal, better noise reduction from outside, and increased overall property value. They also eliminate drafts and make your home more comfortable.

Are vinyl windows a durable choice?

Absolutely. Vinyl windows are highly resilient and require very little maintenance. They do not require painting, resist moisture and decay, and maintain their structural integrity and appearance for decades, making them a highly cost-effective investment.

What makes energy efficient windows different from standard ones?

Energy efficient windows utilize advanced technologies such as Low-E (low-emissivity) glass coatings, multiple panes of glass, and inert gas fills (like argon) between the panes. These features work together to minimize heat transfer, keeping your home warmer in winter and cooler in summer.

Why are double hung windows so popular?

Double hung windows feature two operational sashes that move vertically. This design allows for excellent ventilation control (you can open the top, bottom, or both). Additionally, modern designs allow the sashes to tilt inward, making them incredibly easy to clean from inside the home.

How important is professional window installation?

Professional window installation is crucial. Even the highest quality window will fail to perform if it is installed incorrectly. Expert installers ensure an airtight, watertight fit, proper insulation around the frame, and correct operation, protecting your investment and maximizing energy savings.
