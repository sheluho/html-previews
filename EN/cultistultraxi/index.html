Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about tire care and maintenance.

How often should I schedule a tire rotation?

It is generally recommended to have a tire rotation every 5,000 to 7,000 miles, or at the same time as your regular oil change. This practice helps ensure even tread wear and extends the overall life of your tires.

What is included in your tire installation service?

A standard tire installation includes removing the old tires, mounting the new tires onto the wheels, computerized balancing to prevent vibration, and securing them to your vehicle with the correct torque specifications.

Do I need a wheel alignment when getting a tire replacement?

Yes, it is highly advisable. Getting a proper wheel alignment with your tire replacement ensures that your new tires meet the road at the correct angle, preventing premature and uneven wear right from the start.

How does your seasonal tire storage work?

When you come in to swap your seasonal tires, our partners will clean, label, and safely keep your off-season set in a secure, climate-controlled tire storage facility until you are ready to have them reinstalled the following season.

What are the clear signs that I need new tires?

Key indicators include low tread depth (below 2/32 of an inch), visible cracks or cuts in the sidewall, bulges or blisters on the tire surface, or if you experience excessive vibration while driving. If you notice any of these, a professional inspection is recommended.
