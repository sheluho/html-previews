Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your upcoming kitchen renovation.

What is included in a turnkey kitchen remodeling project?

Our turnkey service is entirely comprehensive. It covers everything from the initial design consultation and 3D rendering to demolition, plumbing, electrical work, flooring, the installation of your custom kitchen cabinets, countertops, and the final walk-through inspections.

Can I choose my own custom kitchen materials?

Absolutely. We believe your kitchen should reflect your distinct taste. We guide you through selecting the finest materials for cabinets, countertops, backsplashes, and fixtures to ensure the final result perfectly matches your vision.

How long does a full-service kitchen renovation take?

Timelines vary based on the complexity and scope of the project. However, most comprehensive remodels are completed within a few weeks to a couple of months. We provide a detailed project schedule before construction begins so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you offer custom kitchen cabinets for unusual layouts?

Yes. One of the main benefits of our full-service approach is that our custom cabinetry solutions are specifically designed and fabricated to maximize space, flow, and functionality in any kitchen layout, no matter how unique or challenging.

When is the best time to start planning my kitchen remodel?

The best time to start is right now. Early planning allows for meticulous design work, unhurried material sourcing, and efficient scheduling. This preparation is key to ensuring a smooth, uninterrupted renovation process once the physical work begins.
