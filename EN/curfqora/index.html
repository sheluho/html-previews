Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our residential moving company and services.

Are you equipped to handle long distance moves?

Yes, our long distance movers are fully equipped to transport your belongings across state lines. We provide dedicated trucks, detailed inventory management, and transparent timelines to ensure your interstate relocation is as smooth as possible.

Do you provide packing materials and packing services?

Absolutely. We offer comprehensive packing services which include high-quality corrugated boxes, bubble wrap, packing paper, and specialized crates for fragile items. You can choose full-service packing or partial packing for specific rooms.

How far in advance should I book local movers?

For local moves, we generally recommend booking at least two to four weeks in advance. During peak moving seasons (summer months and month-ends), reaching out to our residential moving company even earlier is highly advisable to secure your preferred date.

How do you protect my furniture and home during the move?

Our movers use heavy-duty moving blankets, shrink wrap, and floor runners to protect both your belongings and your property. We take extra precautions around door frames, staircases, and hardwood floors to prevent any damage during the loading and unloading processes.

Do you handle specialty items like pianos or antiques?

Yes, our residential moving company is trained to handle delicate and heavy specialty items. We utilize specific equipment and techniques to ensure antiques, fine art, and large instruments are moved securely. Please notify us of these items during your initial consultation.
