Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose fiberglass doors for my entryway?

Fiberglass doors offer incredible durability and require very little maintenance. They mimic the rich texture of real wood but won't warp, crack, or rot when exposed to harsh weather, making them an ideal choice for a lasting front door replacement.

Do you handle both entry doors and interior doors?

Yes, we are a comprehensive entry and interior doors installation company. We can upgrade your home's exterior security and curb appeal, as well as replace bedroom, bathroom, and closet doors to unify your home's interior design.

How long does a typical door installation take?

A standard front door replacement usually takes a few hours to complete, ensuring your home is secure by the end of the day. Interior doors can often be installed more quickly, though whole-house interior upgrades may take a few days depending on the scope.

Do I need to replace the frame when getting a new door?

It depends on the condition of your current frame. If the existing frame is square, plumb, and free of rot, we can often install a slab door. However, for maximum energy efficiency and security, especially with entry doors, a pre-hung unit (which includes a new frame) is highly recommended.

Who actually performs the labor?

Curledge operates as an independent promotional partner. All physical door installations, consultations, and contracting work are handled directly by the licensed professionals at Peter L. Brown, ensuring you receive top-tier, guaranteed craftsmanship.
