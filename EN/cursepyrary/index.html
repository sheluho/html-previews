Common Questions

What is the difference between blinds and shades? ▼

Blinds are typically made of hard materials like wood, faux wood, or aluminum and feature slats that can be tilted to control light. Shades, such as roller shades or cellular shades, are made from continuous panels of fabric or woven materials that roll or fold up and down.

Are plantation shutters suitable for high-humidity areas? ▼

Yes, while traditional wood shutters are beautiful, composite or faux wood plantation shutters are highly recommended for areas like bathrooms or kitchens. They resist moisture, preventing warping or cracking over time.

How do I know which window treatments are right for my home? ▼

Choosing the right window treatments depends on your needs for privacy, light control, insulation, and aesthetics. We recommend scheduling a consultation where professionals can assess your space and guide you through options like custom drapes or motorized shades.

Do you provide measuring and installation services? ▼

As a promotional partner, we connect you with the providers at Savannah Blinds who handle complete end-to-end services. Their professional team conducts precise measurements and handles the full installation to ensure a perfect fit for your custom window treatments.

Can custom drapes be layered with other window coverings? ▼

Absolutely. Layering custom drapes over roller shades or blinds is a popular design choice. It provides maximum versatility for light control while adding depth, texture, and a luxurious finish to the room's decor.
