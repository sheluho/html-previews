Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book a wedding photographer?

We recommend booking your wedding photographer at least 9 to 12 months in advance. Popular dates, especially during the spring and fall seasons, tend to fill up quickly. Early booking ensures we are available for your special day.

Do you offer destination wedding photography services?

Yes, absolutely! We love traveling to capture unique celebrations. Our destination wedding photography packages are custom-tailored to cover travel and accommodation, ensuring you get our signature style anywhere in the world.

Can we include engagement photos in our package?

Most of our comprehensive collections include a complimentary session for engagement photos. We highly encourage this as it allows us to build a relationship and helps you feel more comfortable in front of the camera before the wedding.

How long does it take to receive our final gallery?

You can typically expect a small preview gallery within a few days of your wedding. The fully edited, high-resolution final gallery is usually delivered within 6 to 8 weeks, as we take great care in editing each image to perfection.

Do you offer standalone sessions for bridal portraits?

Yes, we do. Dedicated bridal portraits are a wonderful way to capture detailed, relaxed photos in your attire without the time constraints of the actual wedding day. These can be booked as an add-on or a standalone session.
