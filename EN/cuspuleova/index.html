Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do the caterers you feature handle? ▼

We showcase providers who specialize in a wide range of events. This primarily includes comprehensive wedding catering, high-end corporate catering, private anniversary dinners, and large-scale social galas. No event is too intimate or too grand.

Can we choose between a plated dinner and buffet catering? ▼

Absolutely. The culinary partners we highlight offer flexible service styles. You can opt for a formal, multi-course plated dinner for a traditional feel, or choose extensive buffet catering stations for a more dynamic and interactive guest experience.

How far in advance should we book our event catering? ▼

For peak season events, especially wedding catering, it is highly recommended to secure your caterer 9 to 12 months in advance. For smaller corporate catering needs, a few weeks' notice may suffice, but earlier is always better to ensure availability.

Are dietary restrictions and allergies accommodated? ▼

Yes. Professional event catering services pride themselves on inclusivity. Menus can be fully customized to accommodate vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, and other specific dietary requirements to ensure every guest enjoys a safe and delicious meal.

Do the catering packages include waitstaff and bartenders? ▼

Most comprehensive event catering service packages include professional waitstaff, experienced chefs on-site, and skilled bartenders. Full-service means you won't have to worry about setup, service, or cleanup during your celebration.
