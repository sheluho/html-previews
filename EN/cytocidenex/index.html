Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about professional cleaning services.

How long does carpet cleaning take to dry?

Unlike traditional heavy steam cleaning methods that leave carpets soaked for days, the specialized low-moisture carpet cleaning techniques we promote usually allow your floors to dry in just about an hour. This ensures minimal disruption to your household.

Is professional upholstery cleaning safe for delicate fabrics?

Yes. Professional upholstery cleaning uses specialized equipment and gentle, yet effective, cleaning solutions. Technicians assess the material of your furniture beforehand to determine the safest and most effective method, ensuring no shrinkage or color bleeding occurs.

Can you help with tough pet stain removal?

Absolutely. The service providers we feature utilize advanced stain removal treatments designed specifically for organic stains and pet accidents. These solutions break down the enzymes causing the stain and odor at the source, offering results that standard retail products cannot match.

What is the difference between your methods and standard steam cleaning?

Traditional steam cleaning relies on pumping large amounts of water into the carpet pad, which can lead to mold, mildew, and long dry times. The low-moisture process promoted here uses specialized solutions and equipment to lift dirt efficiently without over-wetting the backing of your carpet.

Do you provide rug cleaning for area and oriental rugs?

Yes, professional rug cleaning is a core service. Whether it is a synthetic area rug or a delicate natural-fiber oriental rug, the technicians apply the appropriate cleaning techniques to remove embedded dust and allergens while protecting the fibers and dyes.
