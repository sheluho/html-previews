Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?
It is highly recommended to schedule routine HVAC maintenance at least twice a year. Ideally, you should have your air conditioner checked in the spring before the summer heat arrives, and your furnace or heating system inspected in the fall before winter temperatures drop.
What are the signs that I need a new air conditioner?
Key indicators that it might be time for a new AC installation include your unit being older than 10-15 years, requiring frequent and expensive repairs, producing unusual noises, or failing to cool your home evenly. A steady increase in your energy bills can also signal declining efficiency.
Is a heat pump a better option than a traditional furnace?
Heat pumps are incredibly versatile because they provide both heating and cooling in a single unit. They are extremely energy-efficient in moderate climates. However, in regions with severe, freezing winters, a traditional furnace may still be necessary, or a dual-fuel system combining both can be utilized.
Does Dacrydaya directly perform the AC installation?
No, Dacrydaya operates as an independent promotional site. We connect you with verified, professional HVAC heating and air conditioning company providers, such as Texas Ace Heating & Air, who handle the actual assessments, installations, and repairs.
How can I improve my overall heating and cooling efficiency?
Simple steps include regularly changing your HVAC air filters every 1 to 3 months, installing a programmable or smart thermostat, ensuring your home is properly insulated, and sealing any drafts around windows and doors to keep treated air inside.
