Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries about our event services.

Do you offer full-service wedding catering?

Yes, the providers we promote offer comprehensive wedding catering services. This includes menu design, food preparation, staffing, and execution for everything from cocktail hours to the main reception dinner.

Can we choose between a plated dinner and buffet catering?

Absolutely. You have complete flexibility. You can opt for an elegant, formal plated dinner where guests are served at their tables, or choose a dynamic buffet catering setup that allows guests to sample a wider variety of dishes at their own pace.

What is included in your corporate catering packages?

Corporate catering packages are highly customizable. They typically range from simple drop-off boxed lunches for boardroom meetings to fully staffed, hot buffet catering stations designed for large company galas or multi-day conferences.

How far in advance should we book for event catering?

For optimal availability, especially during peak seasons, it is recommended to book event catering services at least 6 to 9 months in advance. However, providers can occasionally accommodate shorter timelines depending on the scale of the event and current schedule.

Do you accommodate specific dietary restrictions?

Yes, the culinary teams are highly experienced in accommodating various dietary needs. Whether you require gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, or allergy-specific options integrated into your plated dinner or buffet catering, custom menus can be designed to ensure all guests are safely served.
