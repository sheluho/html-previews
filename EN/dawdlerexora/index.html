Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save by switching to residential solar panels?
Savings vary depending on your current energy usage, local utility rates, and the size of your solar installation. However, most homeowners see a significant reduction in their monthly electricity bills, often saving thousands of dollars over the lifespan of their photovoltaic system.
Is my roof suitable for a solar installation?
Most modern roofs are capable of supporting solar panels. Ideal roofs have a clear, unshaded southern, eastern, or western exposure and are in good structural condition. The installation partners we connect you with will conduct a thorough site evaluation to ensure your roof is a perfect candidate.
How long do photovoltaic panels typically last?
High-quality residential solar panels are incredibly durable and are built to withstand harsh weather conditions. Most tier-one solar panels come with a performance warranty of 25 years, and they often continue generating clean solar power well beyond that timeframe at a slightly reduced efficiency.
Will I still have power during a grid outage?
Standard grid-tied solar systems automatically shut off during a blackout for safety reasons. However, if your solar installation includes a home battery backup system, you can store excess energy and keep your essential appliances running seamlessly during grid outages.
How do I maintain my residential solar system?
Solar panels require very little maintenance. Generally, occasional cleaning to remove dust, leaves, or debris is sufficient to keep them operating at peak efficiency. Most systems also come with robust monitoring software so you can easily track performance and identify any rare issues immediately.
