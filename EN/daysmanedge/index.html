Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom closet installation take?
The timeline for installing custom closets varies depending on the complexity and size of the project. Generally, once the final design is approved and materials are fabricated, the actual installation takes between one to three days. Your dedicated design consultant will provide a precise schedule during the planning phase.
Do you offer solutions for small reach-in closets?
Absolutely. Optimizing limited space is a core specialty. A well-designed reach-in closet utilizes vertical space, double-hanging rods, and specialized closet organizers to significantly increase your storage capacity, making small spaces highly functional.
Can I customize the materials and finishes for my wardrobe?
Yes, customization is at the heart of what is offered. You can choose from a wide array of premium materials, wood grains, solid colors, and hardware finishes to ensure your new wardrobe perfectly matches your home's existing interior design.
What is included in a typical closet organizer system?
A standard closet organizer system often includes a mix of adjustable shelving, varying heights of hanging rods, soft-close drawers, and specialized racks for shoes, ties, or belts. Every system is uniquely configured based on your specific inventory of clothing and accessories.
Do you design walk-in closets for rooms with awkward shapes?
Yes, challenging architectural features like sloped ceilings, alcoves, or non-standard angles are common. Professional designers excel at creating custom walk-in closet layouts that navigate these obstacles, turning awkward corners into valuable, accessible storage areas.
