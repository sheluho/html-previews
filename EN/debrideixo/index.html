Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a turnkey renovation?

A turnkey renovation means that we, as your general contractor, handle the entire scope of the project from start to finish. This includes design consultations, securing necessary municipal permits, purchasing materials, managing all specialized trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC), and conducting the final site cleanup. You simply "turn the key" and walk into your completed space.

How long does whole home remodeling usually take?

Timelines vary significantly based on the square footage, the complexity of structural changes, and material availability. A standard whole home remodeling project can take anywhere from 3 to 6 months of active construction. During our initial consultation, we will provide a detailed schedule outlining each phase of the build.

Do I need to hire an architect for a home addition?

For most structural home additions, architectural plans are required to secure building permits. We partner with licensed architects and structural engineers to draft these plans, ensuring they meet all local building codes while achieving your aesthetic goals. This is all coordinated under our umbrella of services.

Can we live in the house during a home renovation?

It depends on the scope of the project. If we are performing a comprehensive whole home remodeling where essential utilities (water, power, HVAC) will be disrupted for extended periods, we highly recommend making temporary living arrangements. For phased projects or localized additions, it is often possible to remain in the home, and we take strict measures to minimize dust and disruption.

Why should I hire a general contractor instead of managing trades myself?

Managing a home renovation requires extensive industry knowledge to sequence trades correctly, anticipate structural issues, and ensure quality control. A general contractor assumes the liability for the site, ensures all work passes municipal inspections, and leverages established relationships with reliable subcontractors, ultimately saving you time, stress, and costly mistakes.
