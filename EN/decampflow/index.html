Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a ceramic coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied by hand to the exterior of a vehicle. It chemically bonds with the factory paint, creating a layer of protection that won't wash away or break down over a short period of time like traditional wax. It offers immense paint protection against dirt, UV rays, and chemical stains.

How long does an interior detailing service take?

Depending on the condition of the vehicle, a comprehensive interior detailing can take anywhere from 3 to 6 hours. The process includes deep cleaning, steam treatment, stain extraction, and conditioning of all surfaces to ensure a thoroughly restored cabin.

Do I need paint correction before applying a ceramic coating?

Yes, in almost all cases. Ceramic coating seals the paint beneath it. If you apply it over swirl marks or scratches, those defects will be locked in and highly visible. Paint correction ensures the surface is as flawless as possible before the semi-permanent protective layer is applied.

How often should I get my car detailed?

For optimal maintenance, we recommend a full car detailing service twice a year, with regular maintenance washes in between. If your car has a professional ceramic coating, the maintenance becomes significantly easier, but professional check-ups are still advised annually.

Does ceramic coating prevent rock chips?

No. While ceramic coating provides excellent resistance against light scratches, chemical etching, and UV fading, it is not thick enough to absorb the impact of a rock chip. For heavy impact protection, Paint Protection Film (PPF) is the recommended solution.
