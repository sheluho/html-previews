Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding the wedding photography services we promote.

How far in advance should we book our wedding photographer?

It is highly recommended to secure your wedding photographer at least 9 to 12 months in advance. Popular dates during peak wedding seasons book quickly, so early inquiry ensures availability for your preferred date.

Do you offer engagement photos as part of your packages?

Yes, the providers we connect you with frequently include engagement photos within their comprehensive coverage options. These sessions are excellent for building rapport with the photographer before the main event.

Are bridal portraits included or scheduled separately?

Bridal portraits can be captured dynamically on the wedding day itself, or scheduled as a separate, dedicated session prior to the wedding. Dedicated sessions allow for a more relaxed environment and varied locations.

Do you travel for destination wedding photography?

Absolutely. The studio professionals are experienced in destination wedding photography and are available for travel globally. Custom travel quotes are provided based on the specific location and logistical requirements of your event.

How many images can we expect from our wedding day?

The final image count varies based on the hours of coverage and the flow of the event. On average, full-day coverage yields a robust gallery of fully edited, high-resolution images that comprehensively tell the story of your day.
