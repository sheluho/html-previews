Frequently Asked Questions

How long does carpet cleaning take to dry? ▼

Typically, carpets cleaned using our steam cleaning extraction method take about 4 to 8 hours to dry completely. Drying times can vary based on the humidity, airflow, and temperature in your home.

Do you offer upholstery cleaning for all fabric types? ▼

We clean a wide variety of fabrics, including synthetics, cotton blends, and microfibers. Before beginning any upholstery cleaning, we inspect the material to determine the safest and most effective cleaning method.

What exactly is steam cleaning? ▼

Steam cleaning, also known as hot water extraction, involves injecting heated water and specialized cleaning agents deep into the carpet fibers, then immediately extracting the water along with the dislodged dirt and allergens.

Can you guarantee complete stain removal? ▼

While we use advanced stain removal techniques and have a high success rate, we cannot guarantee the removal of every stain. Factors such as the stain's age, the type of spill, and previous DIY cleaning attempts all affect the outcome.

How often should I schedule rug cleaning? ▼

For normal household traffic, we recommend professional rug cleaning every 12 to 18 months. Homes with pets, young children, or high foot traffic may benefit from cleaning every 6 to 12 months to maintain appearance and hygiene.
