Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our premium car wash service.

What is included in your premium car wash?
Our premium car wash includes a comprehensive exterior foam wash, meticulous hand drying, rim cleaning, tire shine application, and exterior window cleaning to ensure a flawless finish.
How long does a hand car wash typically take?
A standard hand car wash usually takes between 30 to 45 minutes, depending on the size and condition of your vehicle. We prioritize quality and precision over rushing the process.
Can I upgrade to an auto wash membership later?
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade to our auto wash membership at any time to start enjoying recurring benefits, unlimited washes, and exclusive priority lane access.
Is the express wash safe for my car's paint?
Completely safe. We utilize advanced soft-touch materials, highly filtered water, and pH-balanced soaps to ensure your paint is protected while dirt and grime are effectively removed.
Do I need an appointment for a wash?
No appointment is necessary for our standard or express washes. However, auto wash membership holders do receive priority lane access during peak hours for faster service.
