Frequently Asked Questions

What types of window treatments do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of solutions including custom blinds, roller shades, plantation shutters, and bespoke custom drapes. Each product line comes in various materials, colors, and opacities to suit your specific interior design needs.

Are your roller shades suitable for large or oversized windows?

Yes, our roller shades can be customized to fit large, expansive windows. We utilize heavy-duty hardware and premium fabrics that maintain their shape and functionality even across wide spans, offering seamless light control.

How do I choose between custom blinds and plantation shutters?

Choosing between the two depends on your aesthetic preference and functional needs. Custom blinds offer versatile light filtering and a lighter visual footprint. Plantation shutters act as a permanent architectural upgrade to your home, offering superb insulation and a classic, substantial look.

Do you provide custom drapes for uniquely shaped windows?

Absolutely. Our custom drapes are tailored to your exact specifications. Whether you have arched windows, bay windows, or double-height ceilings, we can design and fabricate a drapery solution that perfectly complements your space's unique architecture.

What is the process for measuring and installation?

The process begins with an in-depth consultation to determine your style and needs. Next, precise measurements are taken to ensure a flawless fit. Once your custom window treatments are fabricated, specialized installers will complete the setup to ensure perfect operation and finish.
