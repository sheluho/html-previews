Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a reach-in closet and a walk-in closet?

A reach-in closet is typically a shallower storage space where you reach in to access your items, common in smaller bedrooms or hallways. A walk-in closet provides enough physical space for you to step inside, offering significantly more square footage for specialized shelving, islands, and extensive hanging areas.

Can you build a custom closet organizer for an existing space?

Absolutely. We specialize in transforming existing standard closets by installing a highly efficient custom closet organizer. This approach maximizes your current layout, adding multiple tiers for hanging, drawers for accessories, and adjustable shelving.

How long does the design and installation process take?

Timelines vary based on the complexity of your custom wardrobes. Generally, the initial design phase takes a few days. Once the design is approved, manufacturing and final installation are typically completed within a few weeks, ensuring minimal disruption to your home.

What materials do you use for custom wardrobes?

We use a variety of premium, highly durable materials tailored to your aesthetic preferences and budget. This includes solid woods, high-quality engineered wood composites, glass accents, and robust metal hardware to ensure longevity and elegance.

Do I need to empty my closet before the installation?

Yes, we require the space to be completely cleared of clothing and existing loose storage items before our installation team arrives. This ensures a safe, efficient workspace for our craftsmen to build your new custom closets.
