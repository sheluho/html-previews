Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding auto detailing and ceramic coating.

What is the difference between auto detailing and a regular car wash?
A regular car wash primarily removes loose surface dirt and is often an automated process. Auto detailing is a meticulous, labor-intensive manual process that cleans, restores, and protects both the interior and exterior of the vehicle, addressing embedded contaminants and minor imperfections.
How long does a ceramic coating last?
The longevity of a ceramic coating depends on the specific product applied, environmental conditions, and how well the vehicle is maintained. Generally, professional-grade ceramic coatings can provide robust paint protection lasting anywhere from 2 to 5 years, significantly outperforming traditional waxes.
Is paint correction necessary before applying a ceramic coating?
Yes, it is highly recommended. A ceramic coating forms a semi-permanent, transparent bond over your clear coat. If applied over swirls or scratches, those defects will be locked in and magnified. Paint correction ensures the surface is as flawless as possible before the protective layer is added.
What does interior detailing include?
Comprehensive interior detailing usually involves deep vacuuming, steam cleaning or shampooing of carpets and upholstery, cleaning and conditioning of leather surfaces, and thorough wiping and UV protection of all hard plastics, vinyl, and dashboard components to restore a factory-fresh feel.
How often should I have my car detailed?
For optimal preservation, a full auto detailing is recommended 2 to 3 times a year. However, this depends on your driving habits, where the car is parked, and whether you have applied long-term paint protection like a ceramic coating, which can reduce the frequency of intensive exterior details needed.
