Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a turnkey renovation?

A turnkey renovation means the general contractor handles every single aspect of your project from start to finish. This includes design, obtaining permits, ordering materials, construction, and final cleanup. You only have to turn the key and walk into your finished, ready-to-use space without managing multiple subcontractors yourself.

How long does whole home remodeling typically take?

Timelines vary greatly depending on the scope of the project, the size of the home, and material availability. A full gut whole home remodeling project can take anywhere from 4 to 8 months or more. Your general contractor will provide a detailed, phased timeline during the initial planning stages.

Do I need to move out during a major home renovation?

For extensive whole home remodeling where essential utilities (water, power, HVAC) will be shut off for extended periods, it is highly recommended to arrange alternative living accommodations. However, for phased projects or smaller home additions, it is often possible to remain in the home while work is isolated to specific areas.

Can you help with designing my home addition?

Yes, as part of a turnkey approach, the provider works with architectural designers to plan your home addition. They ensure the new space not only meets your functional needs but also integrates perfectly with the existing style and structural requirements of your home.

Why should I hire a general contractor instead of managing trades myself?

A professional general contractor brings crucial project management expertise, established relationships with reliable subcontractors, and deep knowledge of local building codes. They assume the liability, manage the daily scheduling, handle unexpected structural issues, and ensure the entire project stays cohesive and on budget.
