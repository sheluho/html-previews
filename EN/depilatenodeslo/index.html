Frequently Asked Questions

How long does carpet cleaning take to dry?

Thanks to our modern low-moisture technology, carpets typically dry in just 1 to 2 hours, rather than the 12 to 24 hours required by traditional steam cleaning methods.

Do you offer upholstery cleaning for delicate fabrics?

Yes, our technicians carefully assess your furniture's fabric before beginning any work. We use specialized, gentle solutions designed to effectively clean and protect delicate upholstery.

Is your stain removal process safe for pets and children?

Absolutely. We prioritize the health of your home by using eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning solutions that are tough on stains but completely safe for your family and pets.

Can you clean area rugs in my home?

Yes, our rug cleaning services are performed conveniently in your home. Our equipment is designed to thoroughly clean your area rugs right where they sit, protecting the floors underneath during the process.

What is the difference between your method and traditional steam cleaning?

Traditional steam cleaning can leave carpets overly saturated, which risks mildew and long dry times. Our advanced process uses an encapsulating solution that requires up to 95% less water, pulling dirt up and away from the fibers for a superior, fast-drying clean.
