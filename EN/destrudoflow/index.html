Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our wedding planning services.

What exactly does a full-service wedding planner do?

A full-service wedding planner handles the entirety of your wedding process from concept to completion. This includes budget creation, venue scouting, vendor contract negotiations, design and styling, timeline management, and overseeing the entire event on the day itself. We act as your primary point of contact for all things related to the wedding.

Do I really need a day of coordinator if my venue provides a manager?

Yes. A venue manager is responsible for the venue's facilities and staff, ensuring the building runs smoothly. A dedicated day of coordinator works exclusively for you. We manage your specific timeline, cue the bridal party, handle your personal items, coordinate external vendors (like photographers and florists), and troubleshoot personal details that a venue manager does not oversee.

How do you handle a destination wedding?

Planning a destination wedding involves complex logistics, from navigating local legal requirements for marriage to sourcing reliable vendors in a different country or state. We leverage our network and handle all communication, travel logistics recommendations, and on-site coordination to ensure your distant celebration feels as seamless as a local one.

When should we ideally hire our wedding coordinator?

For full-service planning, we recommend reaching out 12 to 18 months before your desired date. If you have already secured your major vendors and are looking specifically for a day of coordinator, we suggest booking us 6 to 9 months in advance so we can begin reviewing your contracts and building your final timeline effectively.

How is your wedding planning priced?

Every couple's needs are distinct. We provide customized quotes based on the scale, location, and complexity of your event. Whether you require an extensive full-service package with multi-day event management or a streamlined coordination package, we will discuss your requirements during our initial consultation and outline a transparent fee structure.
