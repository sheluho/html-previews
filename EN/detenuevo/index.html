Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning custom furniture.

What is the process for ordering custom furniture?

The process begins with a consultation to understand your vision, space constraints, and material preferences. Following this, detailed designs and dimensions are drawn up for your approval. Once finalized, our partner craftspeople begin building your bespoke furniture in their workshop.

How long does it take to build a custom dining table?

Lead times vary depending on the complexity of the design and current workshop schedules. Typically, a custom dining table takes between 8 to 12 weeks from the final design approval to completion. We provide estimated timelines during the initial consultation.

Can I choose my own fabric for a custom sofa?

Absolutely. We offer a wide curated selection of premium upholstery options. If you are working with an interior designer or have a specific material in mind, we also accept COM (Customer's Own Material) for your custom sofa projects, ensuring it matches your exact aesthetic.

What materials are used in your handmade furniture?

We emphasize the use of high-quality, sustainable materials. This includes ethically sourced domestic hardwoods like walnut, oak, and maple, as well as historic reclaimed woods salvaged from local structures. Finishes are chosen to enhance the natural grain while providing durable protection.

Do you work with interior designers and architects?

Yes, a significant portion of our bespoke furniture projects involve collaborating with trade professionals. We welcome interior designers and architects to bring us their custom concepts, and we work closely with them to execute their vision with precision craftsmanship.
