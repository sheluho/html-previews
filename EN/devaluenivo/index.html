Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a standard bathroom renovation take?

The timeline for a bathroom renovation varies based on the scope of the project. A straightforward update might take two to three weeks, while a comprehensive, full-scale bathroom remodel involving layout changes and extensive tile installation can take four to six weeks. We provide a detailed schedule during your consultation.

Can you convert my old tub into a walk-in shower?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most popular services. We can remove your existing tub and install a beautiful, spacious walk-in shower customized with safety features, built-in seating, and premium enclosures to maximize your space.

Do you manage the plumbing and electrical work?

Absolutely. As a full-service bathroom remodeling company, we coordinate all necessary trades. Whether it's relocating pipes for a bathtub replacement or upgrading lighting fixtures, our experienced professionals handle all technical aspects to ensure everything is up to code.

What materials do you recommend for tile installation?

We typically recommend porcelain or ceramic tiles for wet areas due to their durability and water resistance. Natural stone is also a stunning option for accents and floors, provided it is properly sealed. We will help you select materials that balance aesthetics, maintenance, and budget.

Will a bathroom remodel increase my home's value?

Yes, updating a bathroom is widely considered one of the best investments you can make in your home. A professional bathroom remodel not only enhances your daily living experience but often yields a strong return on investment by modernizing the property for future buyers.
