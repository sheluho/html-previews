Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a standard house cleaning?
A standard house cleaning covers all the essential tasks to keep your home tidy and sanitary. This typically includes dusting surfaces, vacuuming carpets and rugs, mopping hard floors, sanitizing bathroom fixtures, and wiping down kitchen counters and appliance exteriors.
Do you offer move-out cleaning services?
Yes, comprehensive move-in and move-out cleaning is available. This service is designed to prepare a home for new occupants and involves detailed work such as cleaning inside empty cabinets, wiping baseboards, and thoroughly scrubbing bathrooms and kitchens.
How often should I schedule a recurring cleaning?
The ideal frequency depends on your household's size, lifestyle, and whether you have pets or children. Bi-weekly recurring cleaning is the most popular choice for maintaining a consistently clean home, though weekly or monthly options are also highly requested.
What is the difference between deep cleaning and standard cleaning?
While a standard clean handles everyday dust and dirt, a deep cleaning targets neglected areas. Deep cleaning often involves hand-wiping baseboards, cleaning the interior of windows, dusting high-reach ceiling fans, and tackling accumulated grime behind fixtures.
Are the cleaning products used safe for my family?
The professionals introduced through this platform prioritize the use of safe, effective, and often eco-friendly cleaning solutions. These products are carefully selected to ensure they provide a rigorous clean while remaining safe for children, pets, and the environment.
