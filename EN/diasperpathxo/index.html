Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about auto detailing and paint protection.

What is the difference between a ceramic coating and traditional wax? ▼

Traditional car wax sits on top of the paint and typically lasts only a few months before washing away. Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with the factory clear coat, creating a semi-permanent layer of protection that lasts for years, offering superior resistance to chemicals, UV damage, and minor scratching.

Is paint protection film (PPF) really necessary? ▼

If you want to prevent physical damage like rock chips, deep scratches, and bug splatter etching, PPF is highly recommended. It is a thick, durable urethane film that absorbs impacts that would otherwise chip your paint. It is the most robust form of physical protection available for vehicles.

What does a full interior detailing include? ▼

A comprehensive interior detail involves a thorough cleaning of all cabin surfaces. This typically includes vacuuming, deep hot water extraction for carpets and cloth seats, cleaning and conditioning of leather, detailing of the dashboard, center console, and door panels, as well as streak-free interior window cleaning.

How long does a professional auto detailing service take? ▼

The duration depends on the service package. A standard interior and exterior detail might take 3 to 5 hours. However, services involving multi-stage paint correction followed by a ceramic coating application can take anywhere from 1 to 3 days to ensure proper curing and flawless results.

Do I still need to wash my car if it has a ceramic coating? ▼

Yes, regular washing is still required to remove dirt and traffic film. However, the hydrophobic properties of the ceramic coating make the washing process significantly easier and faster. Dirt will not bond as strongly to the surface, and water will bead and sheet off rapidly.
