Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our door installation and replacement services.

What are the benefits of choosing fiberglass doors for an entry?
Fiberglass doors are highly resilient, offering excellent resistance to warping, rotting, and denting compared to traditional wood or steel. They provide superior insulation, helping to lower energy costs, and can be manufactured to mimic the elegant look of natural wood grain.
How long does a typical front door replacement take?
A standard front door replacement usually takes a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity of the frame and whether structural adjustments are needed. Professional door installation ensures the process is efficient, minimizing disruption to your home while guaranteeing a secure, weatherproof fit.
Do you provide both entry doors and interior doors?
Yes, the services promoted cover a full spectrum of residential door needs. This includes high-security exterior entry doors designed for curb appeal and safety, as well as a variety of stylish interior doors to match your home's internal decor.
Is hardware included with the door installation?
Hardware options, including locks, handles, and hinges, are typically discussed during your initial consultation. You can choose from a range of modern, secure hardware sets that complement your new doors, and the installation team will ensure they are perfectly fitted and aligned.
Can I replace just the door slab, or do I need a pre-hung system?
If your existing door frame is square, plumb, and in good condition without signs of rot, a slab replacement might be possible. However, for maximum energy efficiency, security, and a perfect seal, installing a complete pre-hung system is generally recommended by professionals.
