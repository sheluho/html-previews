Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom menus for wedding catering?

Absolutely. We understand that every couple is unique, and your menu should be too. Our culinary team will work with you to design a personalized menu that reflects your tastes, cultural traditions, and the overall theme of your celebration.

What is the difference between a plated dinner and buffet catering?

A plated dinner offers a formal, seated experience where guests are served individual, pre-selected courses directly at their tables, ensuring an elegant and synchronized dining pace. Buffet catering provides a more relaxed atmosphere, allowing guests to choose from a wide variety of dishes presented at stations, encouraging mingling and diverse flavor combinations.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions for corporate catering events?

Yes, accommodating dietary needs is a standard part of our event catering service. We expertly prepare vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergen-friendly options ensuring all your attendees can enjoy a delicious and safe meal without compromising on quality.

How far in advance should we book your event catering service?

For large-scale events and wedding catering, we recommend securing your date 9 to 12 months in advance, particularly for peak seasons. For smaller corporate catering or social gatherings, 2 to 3 months' notice is generally sufficient, though we will always try to accommodate tighter timelines when possible.

Do you provide staffing and rentals along with the food?

Yes, our comprehensive catering services include professional waitstaff, skilled bartenders, and experienced event managers to ensure flawless execution. We can also assist in coordinating essential rentals such as linens, glassware, and tableware to complete your event's aesthetic.
