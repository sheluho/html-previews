Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about embarking on a bathroom renovation project.

How long does a typical full-service bathroom remodel take?

Timelines vary based on the scope of work. A simple bathtub replacement might take just a few days, while a complete bathroom renovation involving plumbing changes, new walk-in showers, and extensive tile installation can take several weeks. Our partners provide detailed schedules before work begins.

Do I need to purchase materials myself?

As a full-service bathroom remodeling operation, the contractors we promote typically handle all material sourcing. They work with trusted suppliers to provide high-quality fixtures, acrylics, and tiles, ensuring everything is on-site when needed for your bathroom remodel.

Can a bathtub be converted into a walk-in shower?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are highly popular. The providers specialize in transforming old tubs into accessible, spacious walk-in showers. This process often includes custom tile installation and modern glass enclosures to completely revitalize the space.

Are the remodeling services guaranteed?

Warranties and guarantees are provided directly by the original service provider (the contractor performing the work). While Dibromidova acts as a promotional partner, we align with companies that stand behind their craftsmanship and materials for your bathroom renovation.

What should I do to prepare for the installation?

Before the installation team arrives, it is generally recommended to clear out personal items from the bathroom and ensure a clear pathway from the entrance to the work area. The contractor will handle the heavy lifting, demolition, and all aspects of the actual bathroom remodel.
