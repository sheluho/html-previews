Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers regarding door installation and replacement.

How long does a front door replacement typically take?
Most front door replacement projects are completed within a single day. Our partnered installation teams arrive with all necessary materials and ensure your home is secure and sealed before leaving the job site.
What are the main benefits of fiberglass doors over traditional wood?
Fiberglass doors are highly resistant to dents, scratches, and extreme weather conditions. Unlike wood, they will not rot, warp, or expand with moisture, making them an incredibly durable and energy-efficient choice for entry doors.
Do you handle full-house interior doors replacement?
Yes. Through our network, you can schedule a comprehensive upgrade of all your interior doors. This ensures a uniform aesthetic throughout your home, improved privacy, and consistent hardware styling.
How do I know if it's time for a new entry door?
Key signs include feeling drafts around the edges, visible cracks or warping in the frame, difficulty opening or closing the door, and elevated energy bills. A professional door installation can resolve these issues permanently.
Is the hardware included with the door installation?
Yes, our service partners offer comprehensive packages that include premium locks, handles, and hinges. You can choose hardware that perfectly matches your new entry doors or interior doors during your consultation.
