Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about finding the right residential moving company.

What is the difference between local movers and long distance movers?

Local movers typically handle relocations within the same city or state, usually charging by the hour based on the size of the crew. Long distance movers manage interstate moves, where pricing is generally determined by the total weight of your belongings and the distance traveled.

Are professional packing services worth the investment?

For many families, yes. Packing services save days of exhausting labor and ensure that delicate or valuable items are packed using industry-standard materials and techniques, significantly reducing the risk of damage during transport.

How far in advance should I book a moving company?

It is recommended to book your residential moving company at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance. If you are moving during peak season (summer months or end of the month), booking 8 weeks ahead is advisable to secure your preferred dates.

Do movers disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Most full-service moving companies offer basic disassembly and reassembly of standard furniture items like beds and dining tables. It is important to confirm this specific service with your chosen provider during the quoting process.

How is Didycodes involved in my move?

Didycodes operates solely as an independent promotional site. We introduce you to reputable services provided by All My Movers. We do not perform the physical moving services ourselves; all logistics, contracts, and customer support are handled directly by the moving company.
