Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your wedding videography packages?

Our core packages include full-day coverage by a dedicated wedding videographer, a highlight cinematic wedding video, and a longer documentary edit of your ceremony and toasts.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes, absolutely. We offer event videography and wedding film services worldwide. Travel fees are calculated based on the destination and accommodation requirements.

How long does it take to receive our wedding film?

Quality editing takes time. You can expect to receive your final cinematic wedding video within 6 to 8 weeks after your event date.

How do you work alongside our photographer?

Our team is highly collaborative. A great wedding videographer knows how to work seamlessly with photographers, ensuring we both get the perfect shot without crowding you or your guests.

Can we get the raw unedited footage?

Yes, raw footage from your wedding and event videography coverage can be added to any package for an additional fee, delivered on a separate hard drive.
