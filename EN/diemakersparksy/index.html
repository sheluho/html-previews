Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about auto glass and windshield replacement.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

Most windshield replacements are completed within one to two hours. The specialized urethane adhesive requires some time to cure properly, so it is highly recommended not to drive the vehicle immediately after the installation to ensure maximum safety and a watertight seal.

Can my windshield be repaired instead of replaced?

Yes, if the damage is minor, such as a small chip or a crack smaller than a dollar bill, windshield repair is often possible. Prompt chip repair prevents the damage from spreading further across the glass and saves you the cost and time of a full windshield replacement.

What type of auto glass do you use for replacements?

The technicians we promote exclusively use high-quality auto glass that meets or exceeds Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) standards. This commitment ensures the structural integrity of your vehicle, proper sensor alignment, and an exact fitment for long-lasting durability.

Does my insurance cover car window replacement?

Many comprehensive auto insurance policies cover auto glass and car window replacement, often with a waived deductible depending on your state's regulations. The service providers we feature can typically assist you in verifying coverage and filing the insurance claim directly.

Is mobile service available for auto glass repair?

Yes, many of the professionals we feature offer convenient mobile auto glass services. Fully equipped vans can come directly to your home, workplace, or another convenient location to perform the necessary windshield replacement or chip repair on-site, saving you a trip to the shop.
