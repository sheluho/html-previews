Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a standard house cleaning?
A standard house cleaning typically includes dusting all surfaces, vacuuming carpets and rugs, sweeping and mopping hard floors, and thoroughly sanitizing bathrooms and kitchens. It is designed as a recurring cleaning service to maintain the general upkeep and hygiene of your home on a regular basis.
How does deep cleaning differ from regular maid service?
While regular maid service focuses on surface-level maintenance, a deep cleaning is much more intensive. It involves scrubbing neglected areas, wiping down baseboards, cleaning the interior of windows, degreasing kitchen appliances, and removing deep-seated dirt and allergens that accumulate over time.
Do I need to be home during the move-out cleaning?
No, you do not need to be present while the team works. Many clients arrange for secure access to the property ahead of time. This allows the professionals to conduct the move-out cleaning efficiently, ensuring the empty space is spotless for the next occupants without disrupting your moving schedule.
Are the cleaning products safe for my family and pets?
Yes, professional cleaning services prioritize the health of your household by using high-quality, specialized products. If you have specific sensitivities or prefer eco-friendly solutions, you can request specialized green cleaning products to ensure a safe environment for both children and pets.
How often should I schedule recurring cleaning?
The ideal frequency depends on your lifestyle, the size of your home, and the presence of pets or children. Most clients find that bi-weekly recurring cleaning strikes the perfect balance for maintaining a tidy home, though busy households often prefer weekly visits to keep everything consistently pristine.
