Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main benefits of custom cabinets over stock options?

Custom kitchen cabinets offer unparalleled flexibility and durability. Unlike stock cabinets which come in fixed sizes, custom builds are designed to fit the exact dimensions of your unique space, eliminating wasted areas. They also allow for complete personalization of wood types, finishes, and interior storage solutions.

Do you offer shaker cabinets in your kitchen design plans?

Yes, shaker cabinets are one of the most popular styles we incorporate into our kitchen design layouts. Their clean lines and timeless appeal make them versatile enough for both traditional and contemporary homes. You can view various shaker door styles during your visit to our kitchen showroom.

How long does the kitchen cabinet manufacturing process take?

Because every order is built from scratch, the manufacturing timeline typically ranges from 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of the design and current material availability. We provide a more accurate schedule once the final design is approved.

Can I see material samples at your kitchen showroom before deciding?

Absolutely. We encourage all prospective clients to visit our kitchen showroom. It is the best place to physically examine different wood species, stain colors, paint finishes, and hardware options to ensure they align with your vision.

Do you help with the overall kitchen design layout?

Yes, professional kitchen design is a core part of our service. Our experts work with you to understand your workflow, storage needs, and aesthetic preferences to draft a layout that maximizes the potential of your space.
