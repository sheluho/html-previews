Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our HVAC services.

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?
Ideally, you should schedule maintenance for your heating and cooling systems twice a year. We recommend an inspection for your air conditioner in the spring and a check-up for your furnace in the fall.
What are the signs I need a new air conditioner?
Key indicators include frequent and costly repairs, rising energy bills despite normal usage, uneven cooling throughout your home, and if your unit is more than 10-15 years old. A modern AC installation can often save money in the long run.
Do you service both furnaces and heat pumps?
Yes, our network of professionals is fully equipped to handle all types of HVAC equipment. This includes traditional gas or electric furnaces as well as modern, high-efficiency heat pump systems.
How can I improve my home's heating and cooling efficiency?
Simple steps include changing your air filters regularly (every 1-3 months), ensuring your vents are unblocked, upgrading to a programmable thermostat, and keeping up with routine professional maintenance.
What is involved in a professional AC installation?
A proper installation involves calculating the exact cooling load required for your home, inspecting existing ductwork for leaks, safely removing the old unit, installing the new system according to manufacturer specifications, and testing for optimal airflow.
