Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a windshield repair or a full replacement?

Generally, if a chip is smaller than a quarter or a crack is less than a few inches long and not located directly in the driver's line of sight, a chip repair is sufficient. Larger, deeper, or spreading damage usually necessitates a full windshield replacement to maintain the vehicle's structural integrity.

Is it safe to drive with a cracked windshield?

No, it is not recommended. A cracked windshield significantly reduces visibility and compromises the structural strength of your car's cabin, which is crucial in the event of a rollover or collision. Prompt auto glass services are essential for your safety.

How long does a standard car window replacement take?

Most car window replacements can be completed in about one to two hours. However, it is typically advised to allow the vehicle to sit for a short period afterward to ensure the urethane adhesive sets properly before driving.

Will my insurance cover auto glass replacement?

Many comprehensive auto insurance policies cover auto glass and windshield replacement, often waiving the deductible for chip repairs. We recommend checking directly with your insurance provider to understand your specific coverage options.

Do you provide mobile auto glass services?

The service providers we partner with frequently offer mobile options, coming directly to your home or workplace to perform the windshield repair or replacement, ensuring minimal disruption to your daily schedule.
