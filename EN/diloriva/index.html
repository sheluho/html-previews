Common Inquiries

Insights into the renovation process.

What is included in a turnkey kitchen remodeling project?

A turnkey kitchen renovation covers every aspect of the project from start to finish. This typically includes initial design consultations, demolition, plumbing and electrical updates, installation of custom cabinets and countertops, flooring, and final finishing touches, allowing you to walk into a completed space.

Do you recommend custom cabinets over pre-fabricated options?

For a truly bespoke space, custom cabinets are highly recommended. They allow you to maximize every inch of your kitchen, accommodate unique architectural features, and select specific wood species, finishes, and hardware that pre-fabricated options simply cannot match in quality or fit.

How do I choose the right countertops for my custom kitchen?

Selecting countertops involves balancing aesthetics with functionality. Quartz offers high durability and low maintenance, while natural stones like granite or marble provide unique, luxurious patterns. Consider your cooking habits and lifestyle when choosing the surface material.

How long does a full kitchen remodeling generally take?

Timelines vary based on the project's complexity, structural changes, and material availability. A standard full remodel can take anywhere from 6 to 12 weeks once construction begins. Proper planning and securing custom materials in advance help ensure the timeline stays on track.

Can I keep my existing layout during a custom kitchen renovation?

Yes, keeping your existing footprint is often a cost-effective strategy. If the current flow works well for you, we can focus the renovation purely on upgrading aesthetics and functionality—installing new custom cabinets, upgrading countertops, and modernizing fixtures without moving major plumbing or electrical lines.
