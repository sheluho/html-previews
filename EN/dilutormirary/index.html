Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our event catering services.

What types of events do you cater?
We specialize in a wide range of gatherings, primarily focusing on wedding catering and corporate catering. We are also well-equipped to handle private parties, galas, and social celebrations of various sizes.
Do you offer both buffet catering and plated dinner options?
Yes, absolutely. We customize the service style to fit the atmosphere of your event. Whether you desire the interactive flow of buffet catering or the formal elegance of a multi-course plated dinner, we design the experience to match your preferences.
Can you accommodate specific dietary restrictions or allergies?
We are highly experienced in creating delicious menus that cater to various dietary needs, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and nut-free requirements. The safety and satisfaction of all your guests are our top priorities.
How far in advance should we book for a wedding?
For wedding catering, we recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance, especially if your date falls during peak wedding season. This allows ample time for menu tastings, detailed planning, and securing your date on our calendar.
Do you provide staff, linens, and tableware?
Our event catering services can be comprehensive. We can coordinate professional waitstaff, bartenders, and culinary teams. We can also assist in arranging rentals for tableware, linens, and other essential equipment to ensure a seamless event.
