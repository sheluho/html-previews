Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about auto glass repair and replacement.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

Most full windshield replacements can be completed in about one to two hours. However, it is recommended to let the vehicle sit for an additional hour to allow the urethane adhesive to properly cure before driving.

When is chip repair possible instead of replacement?

Generally, if a chip or crack is smaller than a quarter and is not in the driver’s direct line of sight, a chip repair is possible. It is best to have it inspected as soon as possible before the damage expands.

Does auto insurance cover car window replacement?

Yes, many comprehensive auto insurance policies cover auto glass and car window replacement. Coverage depends on your specific policy and deductible. It is always recommended to check with your insurance provider.

Are the auto glass materials used high quality?

Absolutely. The auto glass materials used by professional technicians typically meet or exceed Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) standards, ensuring exact fitment, clarity, and maximum safety.

What should I do immediately after my auto glass is replaced?

After a fresh windshield replacement, avoid high-pressure car washes for at least 24 to 48 hours. Keep a window slightly open if possible to prevent air pressure buildup from stressing the new seal as the adhesive cures.
