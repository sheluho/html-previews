Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule lawn mowing services?
For optimal health and appearance, weekly lawn mowing is generally recommended during the peak growing seasons in spring and summer. Bi-weekly schedules may be sufficient during slower growth periods in early spring or late fall.
Do you provide custom garden design?
Yes, comprehensive garden design is a core service. Professionals will assess your property's soil, sunlight, and layout to create a custom plan that incorporates the right mix of flora and hardscaping elements to suit your aesthetic preferences.
What does a typical landscape installation include?
Landscape installation can encompass a wide range of activities, including soil preparation, planting trees and shrubs, laying sod, installing mulch or decorative rock, and constructing elements like retaining walls or pathways based on your approved design.
When is the best time of year for tree service and pruning?
While emergency tree service can be performed year-round, routine pruning is often best done during the dormant season (late winter to early spring) before new growth starts. This minimizes stress on the tree and reduces the risk of disease transmission.
Can you help restore a neglected lawn?
Absolutely. Restoring a neglected lawn usually involves a combination of intense weed control, aeration, overseeding, and specialized fertilization, followed by a strict regimen of proper lawn care and mowing to bring it back to full health.
