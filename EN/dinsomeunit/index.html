Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consider replacing my windows?
You should consider replacement windows if you notice drafts, condensation between panes, difficulty opening or closing them, or unexplained increases in your energy bills. Fading furniture near windows is also a sign that the glass lacks proper UV protection.
What are the benefits of vinyl windows?
Vinyl windows are highly popular because they are incredibly durable, energy efficient, and require virtually no maintenance. Unlike wood, they won't rot, warp, or need repainting, making them a cost-effective long-term investment for your home.
Do you offer double hung windows?
Yes, double hung windows are a staple offering. They feature two operable sashes that move vertically, allowing for superior ventilation control and easy cleaning from inside your home. They fit seamlessly into both traditional and modern architectural styles.
How long does the window installation process take?
While timelines vary based on the scope of the project, a standard window installation can often be completed in one to two days. Professional installers prioritize minimizing disruption to your daily life while ensuring every seal is perfectly set.
What makes a window truly "energy efficient"?
Energy efficient windows typically feature multiple panes of glass separated by gas fills (like argon), low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings to reflect infrared light, and tightly sealed frames. These components work together to drastically reduce heat transfer between the interior and exterior of your home.
