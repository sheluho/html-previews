Frequently Asked Questions

What is your approach as a wedding videographer? ▼

Our approach is highly documentary and unobtrusive. We believe in capturing organic moments as they unfold rather than staging scenes. This ensures your cinematic wedding video feels authentic, emotional, and true to the atmosphere of your special day.

Do you offer event videography outside of weddings? ▼

Yes, we provide comprehensive event videography for a variety of occasions. Whether it is a corporate gala, a brand activation, or a private milestone celebration, we apply the same cinematic techniques and high production values to deliver exceptional visual content.

How long does it take to receive our cinematic wedding video? ▼

Crafting a high-quality wedding film requires meticulous editing, color grading, and sound design. Typically, our clients receive their final films within 8 to 12 weeks following the event, ensuring every detail meets our rigorous artistic standards.

Do you travel for destination weddings? ▼

Absolutely. We frequently travel domestically and internationally to capture love stories. Travel and accommodation logistics are simply factored into a custom proposal based on your specific destination requirements.

How do we secure our date with your wedding videography company? ▼

To secure your date, we require a signed agreement and a retainer fee. Since we take on a limited number of commissions each year to maintain the quality of our work, we recommend reaching out as soon as you have confirmed your venue.
