Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?
It is generally recommended to have your heating and cooling systems inspected at least twice a year. A spring tune-up for your air conditioner and a fall inspection for your furnace ensure optimal performance year-round.
What are the signs I need a new AC installation?
If your air conditioner is over 10-15 years old, requires frequent repairs, struggles to cool your home evenly, or causes unexplained spikes in your energy bills, it may be time to consider a modern, energy-efficient replacement.
Can a heat pump replace both my furnace and air conditioner?
Yes. A heat pump is a highly efficient two-in-one system that provides both heating and cooling. During the summer, it functions like an air conditioner, and in the winter, it reverses the process to draw warmth into your home.
How frequently should air filters be changed?
For standard filters, changing them every 30 to 90 days is ideal. If you have pets, suffer from allergies, or have heavy dust accumulation, you should check and replace them more frequently to maintain good indoor air quality.
Do you provide emergency HVAC services?
Yes, the service network we promote includes priority scheduling for heating and cooling emergencies. Losing heat in the winter or AC in peak summer is critical, and prompt diagnostic responses are typically available.
