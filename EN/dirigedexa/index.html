Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a general contractor do during a remodel? ▼

A general contractor oversees the entire construction process. We manage specialized trades (plumbers, electricians, carpenters), secure necessary building permits, procure materials, and ensure that your home remodeling project stays on schedule and adheres to strict quality standards.

How long does a typical whole home renovation take? ▼

The timeline for a whole home renovation varies widely depending on the scope of work, size of the property, and material availability. On average, comprehensive interior remodels take between 3 to 6 months. We provide a detailed, phased schedule during our initial consultation.

Do you handle the permitting for a home addition? ▼

Yes. As part of our turnkey renovation services, we handle all interactions with local zoning boards and building departments. We submit the architectural plans, secure the necessary permits, and schedule all required municipal inspections for your home addition.

What is meant by a "turnkey renovation"? ▼

A turnkey renovation means we provide a complete, end-to-end solution. From the initial design concept and material selection to the construction and final clean-up, we handle everything. You simply "turn the key" to enter your fully finished, ready-to-live-in remodeled space.

How do I prepare my house for a major remodeling project? ▼

Before construction begins, we recommend clearing personal items, furniture, and valuables from the designated work zones. We will set up dust barriers and protect your flooring. Depending on the scale of a whole home renovation, some clients choose to relocate temporarily for maximum convenience.
