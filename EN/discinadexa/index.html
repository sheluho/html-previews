Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our tire maintenance services.

How often should I schedule a tire rotation?

As a general rule, we recommend a tire rotation every 5,000 to 7,000 miles, or every time you change your engine oil. Regular rotation ensures even tread wear, which significantly extends the overall life of your tires and maintains optimal traction.

Is a wheel alignment necessary when buying new tires?

Yes, we strongly recommend a wheel alignment with every new tire installation. If your vehicle's suspension is misaligned, your new tires will begin to wear unevenly almost immediately. An alignment protects your investment and ensures your vehicle drives straight.

How does your tire storage service work?

When you come in for a seasonal swap, we tag, clean, and inspect your off-season tires. They are then securely transported to our climate-controlled facility where they are kept away from moisture and extreme temperatures until your next appointment.

How do I know if I need a tire replacement?

You should consider a tire replacement if the tread depth is below 2/32 of an inch, if you notice significant cracking or bulging on the sidewalls, or if the tires are more than six years old. Our technicians can perform a quick, free inspection to assess their safety.

Do you balance the tires during installation?

Absolutely. Computerized tire balancing is a standard and crucial part of our tire installation process. Balancing ensures that weight is distributed evenly around the axle, preventing steering wheel vibration and avoiding premature wear on your suspension components.
