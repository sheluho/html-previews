Frequently Asked Questions

What types of window treatments do you offer? ▼
We offer a comprehensive selection including custom blinds, roller shades, premium plantation shutters, and bespoke custom drapes tailored to your specific aesthetic and functional requirements.
Do you provide exact custom measurements? ▼
Yes, precision is key to our process. Every order begins with professional measurements to ensure your window treatments fit flawlessly, regardless of your window's unique shape or size.
How long do plantation shutters take to install? ▼
Because our plantation shutters are custom-manufactured to your exact specifications, timelines can vary. Generally, you can expect installation within a few weeks from the date of final measurement and order placement.
Can roller shades effectively block out all light? ▼
Absolutely. We offer a variety of opacity levels for our roller shades, including specialized blackout fabrics that are perfect for bedrooms, media rooms, or any space requiring maximum light control.
Are your custom blinds safe for children and pets? ▼
Safety is a priority. We offer cordless options across our range of custom blinds and shades, providing peace of mind while maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic in your home.
