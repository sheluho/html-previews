Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about residential relocation.

How far in advance should I book my moving company?

It is generally recommended to book your residential moving company at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance, especially during peak summer months. Early planning ensures availability and gives you ample time to coordinate details with your local or long distance movers.

Do you provide the packing materials?

Yes, when you opt for professional packing services through our affiliated provider, USA Moving Co, high-quality packing materials such as sturdy boxes, bubble wrap, packing paper, and custom crates are fully supplied to ensure maximum protection for your belongings.

What is the difference between local and long distance movers?

Local movers typically handle relocations within the same state or under a specific mileage radius (usually around 50-100 miles) and charge by the hour. Long distance movers manage interstate or cross-country moves, and their pricing is generally based on the total weight of your items and the distance traveled.

Is my property insured during the move?

All physical moving operations are conducted by USA Moving Co, which offers various valuation and liability coverage options. Standard basic liability is typically included, but full-value protection can be arranged directly with the moving provider prior to your relocation date.

Can I pack my own belongings?

Absolutely. You can choose to pack entirely on your own, or request partial packing services where professionals only handle fragile or highly valuable items. The moving provider is flexible and will accommodate the level of assistance you require.
