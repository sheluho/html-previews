Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your wedding videography packages?

Our standard packages typically include comprehensive coverage by a professional wedding videographer, multiple camera setups for the ceremony, high-quality audio recording, and a fully edited cinematic wedding video (highlight reel). Extended documentary edits are also available.

Do you offer extended event videography?

Yes. In addition to the main wedding day, we provide event videography for rehearsal dinners, welcome parties, and post-wedding brunches. This allows us to create a more comprehensive narrative of your entire wedding weekend.

How long does it take to receive our cinematic wedding film?

Because we meticulously color-grade and edit every frame to ensure a premium result, delivery timelines vary based on the season. Generally, you can expect to receive your final films within 8 to 12 weeks after your event date.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely. We frequently travel for destination weddings. Travel fees are calculated based on the location and logistics required. Please inquire with your specific location for a custom quote.

How do you handle audio during the ceremony and speeches?

Audio is half the experience of a great wedding film. We use discreet, professional-grade lavalier microphones on key individuals (like the groom and officiant) and tap into the DJ or band's soundboard to ensure crystal-clear audio for vows and toasts.
