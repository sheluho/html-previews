Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do your local movers cover?

We provide comprehensive local moving services across the greater metropolitan area and surrounding suburbs. If you are moving within a 50-mile radius, our local crews are perfectly positioned to assist you.

Do you offer long distance moving?

Yes, our long distance movers are equipped to handle interstate and cross-country relocations with dedicated trucks. We ensure your items stay safe for the long haul without being mixed with other customers' belongings.

What is included in your packing services?

Our packing services supply all necessary materials including sturdy boxes, bubble wrap, and tape. Our trained team can safely pack your entire home or focus solely on fragile and valuable items based on your preference.

How far in advance should I book my moving company?

We recommend booking at least four to six weeks in advance, especially during the busy summer months. This gives us ample time to schedule the appropriate crew and coordinate your packing services if needed.

Are my belongings protected during the move?

Absolutely. We take great care in wrapping and securing your items. As a professional residential moving company, we pad and stretch-wrap furniture to prevent damage and ensure everything arrives in the same condition it left.
