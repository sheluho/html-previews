Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a typical wedding videography package?

Standard packages generally include full-day coverage by a professional wedding videographer, professional audio capture for vows and speeches, a beautifully edited cinematic highlight reel, and a documentary edit of the ceremony and formalities.

Do the videographers travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely. While rooted in the Los Angeles area, the talented professionals we promote are available for destination weddings worldwide to capture your cinematic wedding video wherever your story takes you.

How long does it take to receive our final wedding film?

Because of the intensive nature of color grading and sound mixing required for a premium wedding film, delivery typically takes between 8 to 12 weeks following the event date.

Is event videography available for non-wedding occasions?

Yes. Premium event videography services are available for corporate events, anniversary parties, engagement dinners, and other significant milestones that require professional documentation.

What is the filming style like?

The style seamlessly blends unobtrusive documentary filming with high-end cinematic storytelling. This ensures natural, candid moments are captured alongside visually striking, beautifully composed shots.
