Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a walk-in closet and a reach-in closet?

A walk-in closet is a dedicated room or large space you can physically enter, often featuring extensive shelving, hanging areas, and sometimes a central island. A reach-in closet is typically shallower, built into a wall where you stand outside and reach in for your garments, making a custom closet organizer essential for maximizing the smaller footprint.

Can a custom wardrobe be designed for unusually shaped rooms?

Absolutely. Custom closets are tailored to the exact dimensions and architectural nuances of your home. Sloped ceilings, tight corners, and unique room shapes are easily accommodated with bespoke shelving and hanging solutions that utilize every available inch.

How long does it take to design and install custom closets?

Timelines vary based on the complexity of the project. Generally, after the initial consultation and design approval, fabrication takes a few weeks. The actual installation is typically completed in one to two days by professional craftsmen, ensuring minimal disruption to your home.

What materials are used for the closet organizers?

The providers featured through Djersagenx utilize high-quality, durable materials including premium wood veneers, thermally fused laminates, and robust metal hardware. You can select from a wide range of finishes and textures to perfectly match your home's interior design.

Do you provide the actual installation services?

Djersagenx is an independent promotional operator. We introduce you to exceptional storage concepts, while the actual design, manufacturing, and installation are handled entirely by our partner, Portland Closet Company. They will handle all aspects of the physical project from start to finish.
