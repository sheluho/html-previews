Frequently Asked Questions

What does a turnkey kitchen renovation include?

A turnkey kitchen project means everything is handled for you from start to finish. It typically includes the initial design, demolition of the old layout, installation of new custom cabinets, countertops, flooring, lighting, plumbing fixtures, and all finishing touches. You simply turn the key and walk into a completed space.

Can I choose the style of my custom kitchen cabinets?

Absolutely. The benefit of custom cabinets is complete flexibility. You can select the wood type, finish, door style, hardware, and internal storage solutions to perfectly match your home's aesthetic and your personal organization needs.

How long does countertop installation typically take?

Once the cabinets are fully installed and templates are created, fabrication usually takes a couple of weeks. The actual countertop installation day is quite fast, often completed within a few hours to a single day, depending on the size and complexity of your kitchen layout.

Do you handle the entire kitchen remodeling process?

As a promotional partner, Doeskin Vision connects you directly with a full-service kitchen renovation company that manages the entire remodeling process. The dedicated contractors will oversee the project timeline, permits, materials, and labor to ensure your project stays on track.

Are the materials used for the remodeling durable?

Yes. The partner providers prioritize high-quality, long-lasting materials. Whether it is solid wood for your cabinets or premium quartz and granite for your countertops, the focus is on creating a beautiful kitchen that withstands the heavy daily use of a busy household.
