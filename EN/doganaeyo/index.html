Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a residential solar panel installation take?

The physical installation of solar panels usually takes 1 to 3 days depending on the size and complexity of your roof. However, the entire process, including site assessment, custom design of the photovoltaic system, and acquiring necessary city permits, can take a few weeks to complete before installation begins.

Can solar panels be installed on a flat roof?

Yes, flat roofs are excellent candidates for residential solar power. Our partners utilize specialized mounting systems, such as solar canopies and tilted racks, to ensure the photovoltaic panels are angled perfectly to capture maximum sunlight while preserving the structural integrity of your roof.

Will residential solar power work during a grid outage?

Standard grid-tied solar panels shut down automatically during a blackout for safety reasons. However, if you include a solar power battery backup system in your installation, your home will continue to safely draw stored clean energy during grid outages.

Are there tax incentives for installing solar panels?

Many homeowners qualify for federal, state, and local tax incentives that significantly offset the initial cost of a residential solar installation. We recommend consulting with a tax professional to understand exactly how these incentives apply to your specific photovoltaic investment.

How much maintenance does a photovoltaic system require?

Modern solar panels require very little maintenance. Because there are no moving parts in a standard residential solar array, periodic cleaning to remove dust or debris and an occasional professional inspection are usually all that is needed to keep your system running efficiently for decades.
