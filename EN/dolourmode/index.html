Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom cabinetry process.

What types of custom cabinets do you offer?

We promote a wide variety of styles manufactured by Kountry Kraft, ranging from traditional raised-panel designs to highly sought-after contemporary shaker cabinets. Every project is made to order, ensuring your kitchen cabinets fit your exact aesthetic and functional requirements.

Can I visit the kitchen showroom to see the finishes?

Yes, visiting the kitchen showroom is highly recommended. It allows you to physically interact with the custom woodwork, examine the durability of the hardware, and view the precise color variations in the cabinet finishes before making a final decision.

Do you help with overall kitchen design?

Absolutely. A successful project requires more than just building boxes. The kitchen design process involves layout optimization, material selection, and ergonomic planning. The professionals at Kountry Kraft work closely with you or your interior designer to ensure perfect execution.

Are shaker cabinets a good choice for modern homes?

Shaker cabinets are incredibly versatile and remain one of the most popular choices for modern and transitional homes. Their clean lines and unadorned flat center panels provide a sleek look that pairs beautifully with contemporary hardware and minimalist kitchen design.

How long does it take to manufacture custom kitchen cabinets?

Because every piece is built entirely from scratch to your specific measurements, manufacturing timelines can vary based on the complexity of the order and seasonal demand. Typically, you can expect a dedicated production schedule that focuses entirely on quality and precision.
