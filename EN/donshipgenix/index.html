Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about residential moving solutions.

How far in advance should I book my movers?

It is generally recommended to book your moving company at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance, especially during the peak summer season. Early booking ensures you secure your preferred date and gives you ample time to coordinate packing services if needed.

Are your promoted packing services customizable?

Yes. The service providers we highlight offer flexible packing options. You can opt for a full-service pack where they handle everything, or a partial pack focusing only on fragile, high-value, or difficult-to-pack items like kitchens and artwork.

Do you feature both local and long distance movers?

Absolutely. Donship Genix promotes comprehensive residential solutions, connecting you with reputable local movers for short-distance transitions and experienced long distance movers equipped for interstate relocations.

How is furniture protected during transit?

Professional moving companies utilize heavy-duty moving blankets, shrink wrap, and specialized padding to protect your furniture from scratches, dents, and dirt while being loaded, transported, and unloaded at your new residence.

What should I do before the moving crew arrives?

Before the crew arrives, ensure that all pathways are clear of obstacles. If you are handling your own packing, ensure all boxes are sealed and labeled. It is also advised to keep important personal documents, keys, and essential medications with you personally rather than loading them onto the truck.
