Frequently Asked Questions

Details about our process and services.

Do you offer destination wedding photography?

Yes, we frequently travel for destination wedding photography. We work with couples globally to capture their celebrations, whether it's an intimate elopement in the mountains or a grand event overseas. Custom travel quotes are provided during the consultation.

When should we schedule our engagement photos?

We recommend scheduling your engagement photos 6 to 9 months before your wedding date. This allows ample time to use the images for save-the-dates or a wedding website, and gives us a wonderful opportunity to get comfortable working together before the main event.

What is included in a typical wedding photography collection?

Most of our collections include full-day coverage by a lead wedding photographer, a curated and professionally edited high-resolution digital gallery, and personal print rights. We also offer options to add second shooters, heirloom albums, and dedicated bridal portraits.

How long does it take to receive our final gallery?

We pride ourselves on a meticulous editing process to ensure your images meet our editorial standards. You can typically expect a small preview gallery within a week of the event, with the complete, fully edited gallery delivered within 6 to 8 weeks.

Do you provide direction for posing?

Absolutely. While we heavily favor a candid, photojournalistic approach for much of the day, we provide gentle, natural direction during portraits. Whether it's during engagement photos or formal bridal portraits, our goal is to make you feel relaxed so your true personality shines through.
