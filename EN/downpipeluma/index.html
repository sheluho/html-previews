Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our premium wash processes and memberships.

Why is a hand car wash better than an automated machine?
Automated machines often use harsh bristles and recycled water that can trap abrasive dirt, leading to micro-scratches and swirl marks on your clear coat. Our premium car wash relies exclusively on hand techniques, using fresh microfiber materials and dedicated wash methods to safely lift dirt away from the paint surface without causing damage.
What does the auto wash membership include?
Our auto wash membership is designed for ultimate convenience and vehicle maintenance. It typically includes unlimited access to our express wash services, priority scheduling for deeper detailing sessions, and exclusive member-only discounts on premium add-ons like ceramic coatings and deep interior extractions.
How long does a premium car wash take?
Quality takes time. A standard express wash usually takes about 30 to 45 minutes, as everything is done carefully by hand. A full premium car wash with interior detailing can take anywhere from 1.5 to 3 hours, depending on the size and condition of the vehicle. We recommend booking in advance to ensure dedicated time for your car.
Do you clean and condition leather interiors?
Yes, interior care is a crucial part of our detailing packages. We use specialized pH-balanced cleaners to safely remove dirt and oils from leather seats and trim, followed by high-quality conditioners that nourish the leather, prevent cracking, and restore a soft, matte finish without leaving a greasy residue.
What kind of products do you use for detailing?
We source only professional-grade, environmentally safe detailing products. From our biodegradable, high-lubricity soaps to our premium carnauba waxes and advanced synthetic sealants, every product is selected to provide maximum cleaning power while being completely safe for your vehicle's specific finishes and delicate surfaces.
