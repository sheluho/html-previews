Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical walk-in shower installation take?

The timeline for a walk-in shower installation varies depending on the complexity of the project and existing plumbing. However, many standard installations can be completed efficiently, often minimizing disruption to your daily routine. We prioritize swift, high-quality work to get you back to enjoying your space.

Can you convert my existing bathtub into a walk-in shower?

Yes, bathtub to shower conversions are a highly requested service. We can remove your old, bulky tub and replace it with a sleek, accessible walk-in shower that fits perfectly into the existing footprint, instantly updating the look and feel of your bathroom.

What materials do you use for tile installation?

We offer a wide variety of premium materials for tile installation, including ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and glass. Our experts will help you select the ideal materials that match your aesthetic preferences while providing the durability and water resistance necessary for a bathroom environment.

Are your bathroom remodels designed for accessibility?

Absolutely. We specialize in designing bathrooms that are both beautiful and safe. Features like low-threshold walk-in showers, grab bars, built-in seating, and non-slip tile installation can be seamlessly integrated into your bathroom renovation to ensure long-term comfort and accessibility.

Do I need to obtain permits for a bathroom renovation?

Requirements for permits depend on the scope of your bathroom remodel and local regulations. Major plumbing or electrical changes typically require permits. The professional contractors we connect you with will handle all necessary permitting processes to ensure your project complies with local building codes.
