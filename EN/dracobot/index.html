Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my HVAC system undergo maintenance?

It is widely recommended to schedule professional maintenance for your heating and cooling systems at least twice a year. Ideally, air conditioner units should be serviced in the spring, and your furnace or heat pump should be inspected in the fall before heavy usage begins.

What are the signs I need a new AC installation?

Key indicators that you may need a completely new AC installation include frequent breakdowns, rising energy bills, uneven cooling throughout your home, or if your current air conditioner is more than 10-15 years old and uses outdated refrigerants.

Are heat pumps effective in very cold climates?

Modern heat pumps are incredibly efficient and capable of extracting heat from the air even in freezing temperatures. However, in extreme cold, a dual-fuel system combining a heat pump with a gas furnace is often recommended for optimal efficiency and comfort.

Do you directly perform the heating and cooling services?

No, Dracobot is a promotional platform. We connect you with a verified, independent HVAC heating and air conditioning company that handles all technical assessments, equipment installations, and maintenance services directly.

How can I improve my home's indoor air quality?

Upgrading your HVAC system's air filters regularly, ensuring your ductwork is clean and properly sealed, and integrating specialized air purifiers or humidifiers into your existing heating and cooling setup can drastically improve your indoor air quality.
