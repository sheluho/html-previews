Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer both buffet catering and plated dinner options?

Yes, the service accommodates both styles. You can choose a structured, elegant plated dinner which is perfect for formal weddings, or opt for versatile buffet catering which allows guests more variety and works wonderfully for corporate events and large parties.

How far in advance should we book wedding catering?

It is highly recommended to secure your wedding catering at least 6 to 9 months in advance. Peak wedding seasons fill up quickly, and early booking ensures ample time for menu tastings, finalizing guest counts, and coordinating logistics.

Can you accommodate specific dietary restrictions and allergies?

Absolutely. The culinary team is experienced in preparing dishes that are gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, or tailored to specific food allergies. Please communicate these requirements during the planning phase so menus can be adjusted safely.

What is included in your corporate catering packages?

Corporate catering packages typically include the food preparation, professional delivery and setup, serving equipment (like chafing dishes for buffets), and cleanup of the catered area. Customized packages can also include on-site service staff and beverage stations.

Do you provide tasting sessions before we commit?

Yes, tasting sessions are a standard part of the event catering planning process, particularly for large events like weddings. This allows you to sample the menu options, discuss presentation styles, and make necessary flavor adjustments prior to the big day.
