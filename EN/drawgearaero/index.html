Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full windshield replacement take?

A standard windshield replacement typically takes about 60 to 90 minutes to complete. However, the adhesive used to secure the glass requires additional time to cure before the vehicle is safe to drive. We generally recommend waiting at least one hour after the installation is finished before driving.

Can my auto glass be repaired instead of replaced?

Yes, if the damage is a minor rock chip or a crack smaller than a dollar bill, a chip repair is often possible. The damage must also not be directly in the driver's primary line of sight. Repairing is faster and helps maintain the original factory seal of your windshield.

Do you assist with insurance claims for car window replacement?

The original service providers we promote typically work with all major insurance networks. In many cases, if you have comprehensive coverage, windshield repair might be fully covered with no out-of-pocket cost. Replacement costs will depend on your specific deductible.

Is it safe to wash my car right after new auto glass is installed?

It is highly recommended to wait at least 24 to 48 hours before taking your vehicle through a high-pressure car wash. The high pressure and harsh detergents can damage the fresh urethane seal before it is completely cured, potentially causing leaks.

What type of glass is used for side window replacement?

Unlike the laminated glass used for windshields, side and rear windows are made of tempered glass. Tempered glass is heat-treated to be highly durable and is designed to shatter into small, blunt pieces rather than sharp shards, minimizing the risk of injury during an impact.
