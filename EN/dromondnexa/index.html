Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding professional house cleaning services.

What is included in a standard house cleaning?

A standard cleaning typically includes dusting surfaces, wiping down kitchen counters and appliance exteriors, cleaning bathrooms (sinks, toilets, tubs), vacuuming carpets, and mopping hard floors. It is designed for regular upkeep.

How does deep cleaning differ from regular service?

Deep cleaning is a much more intensive process. It addresses areas not typically covered in a regular visit, such as hand-wiping baseboards, cleaning inside ovens and refrigerators, scrubbing grout lines, and removing heavy buildup in bathrooms and kitchens.

Do I need to provide my own cleaning supplies?

Professional maid services generally arrive fully equipped with all necessary cleaning agents, tools, and vacuums. If you have specific product preferences or allergies, you can usually request that they use supplies you provide.

What should I expect from a move-out cleaning?

Move-out cleaning is designed to return an empty property to a pristine state. It typically involves thorough interior cleaning of all cabinets and drawers, detailed scrubbing of all fixtures, sweeping and mopping of all floors, and ensuring the space is ready for final inspection.

How often should I schedule recurring cleaning?

The frequency depends entirely on your household's needs, size, and lifestyle. Weekly service is ideal for busy families, bi-weekly is the most common for general maintenance, and monthly service works well for low-traffic homes that just need an occasional thorough refresh.
