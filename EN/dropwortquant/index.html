Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How long does it take to design and install custom closets?
The timeline varies depending on the complexity of the project. Typically, the initial consultation and design phase take about a week. Once the design is finalized and materials are ordered, fabrication takes a few weeks. The actual installation of a standard reach-in closet or walk-in closet usually takes just 1 to 2 days.
Can you build a wardrobe for a room with angled ceilings?
Absolutely. One of the main advantages of bespoke custom closets is our ability to work around unique architectural features. We regularly design and install custom storage solutions for attics, under-stair spaces, and rooms with sloped or angled ceilings to maximize every inch of available space.
What materials do you use for your closet organizer systems?
We utilize a variety of high-quality materials to suit different aesthetics and budgets. This includes premium commercial-grade melamine, thermally fused laminates, and solid wood veneers. All hardware, from drawer glides to hanging rods, is selected for durability and smooth operation over years of daily use.
Do I need to empty my existing closet before the consultation?
It is not strictly necessary to empty your closet for the initial design consultation. In fact, seeing your current items helps our designers understand your specific storage needs, such as the ratio of long hanging garments to shoes and folded items. However, the space will need to be completely cleared prior to installation day.
Do you offer lighting integration within the wardrobe?
Yes. Proper lighting transforms a good closet into a spectacular one. We can seamlessly integrate custom LED ribbon lighting, illuminated closet poles, and automated puck lights into your walk-in closet or wardrobe design, ensuring excellent visibility and adding a sophisticated, high-end ambiance to the space.
