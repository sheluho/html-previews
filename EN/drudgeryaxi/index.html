Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a premium car wash?
A premium car wash typically includes a comprehensive exterior wash, advanced clear coat protectants, tire shine, and an undercarriage rinse to ensure your vehicle is thoroughly cleaned and protected from top to bottom.
Do you recommend a hand car wash for luxury vehicles?
Absolutely. A hand car wash is ideal for luxury and classic vehicles with delicate finishes. It utilizes soft, clean mitts and specialized techniques to prevent swirl marks and ensure the highest level of care for your paint.
How does the auto wash membership work?
The auto wash membership allows you to pay a single, flat monthly fee for unlimited washes at the original provider's facility. It's the most cost-effective way to maintain your vehicle's appearance throughout the year.
Is the express wash safe for my paint?
Yes, the express wash utilizes modern, soft-touch materials and high-quality, pH-balanced soaps that are designed specifically to lift dirt safely without damaging your vehicle's clear coat.
How long does a standard wash take?
An express wash typically takes only 5 to 10 minutes from start to finish. A premium hand car wash will take longer, generally around 30 to 45 minutes, depending on the level of detail requested.
