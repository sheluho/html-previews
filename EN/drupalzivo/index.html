Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about your upcoming bathroom renovation.

What does a full bathroom remodel entail?

A full bathroom remodel typically involves completely gutting the existing space. It encompasses new tile installation, plumbing updates, vanity replacement, upgrading fixtures, and installing a new walk-in shower or bathtub replacement to fully transform both function and aesthetics.

How long does a walk-in shower installation take?

Depending on the complexity of the plumbing and the materials selected, a dedicated walk-in shower installation can often be completed in as little as one to three days by professional contractors.

Can I convert my existing tub into a shower?

Absolutely. Tub-to-shower conversions are highly popular for those seeking better accessibility and a modern look. The process involves safely removing the old tub and fitting a custom walk-in shower pan and enclosure in the existing footprint.

What materials are best for a bathtub replacement?

High-quality, multi-layer acrylic is currently one of the most recommended materials for a bathtub replacement. It is non-porous, naturally resists mold and mildew, retains heat well, and avoids the chipping common with older cast iron or fiberglass models.

Do you offer customized tile installation?

Yes, the partners we connect you with specialize in intricate, personalized tile installation. Whether you want a simple ceramic subway tile or elaborate mosaic patterns for your shower walls and bathroom floors, they can execute your exact design vision.
