Frequently Asked Questions

Are solar panels suitable for every type of roof?

While most roofing materials can accommodate solar installation, the ideal setup depends on the age, material, and structural integrity of your roof. The professionals we connect you with will conduct a thorough assessment to determine compatibility before proceeding with any photovoltaic design.

How long does a typical residential solar installation take?

The physical installation of solar panels usually takes between one to three days. However, the entire process—including design, engineering, securing permits, and final utility inspections—can take several weeks to a few months depending on local regulations.

Do solar panels work during cloudy days or winter months?

Yes, solar power systems continue to generate electricity even on cloudy or cold days, though production will be lower than on a perfectly clear, sunny day. Modern photovoltaic technology is highly efficient at capturing indirect sunlight.

What maintenance is required for a residential solar system?

Residential solar systems require very little maintenance. Generally, keeping the panels clear of heavy debris or excessive dust is sufficient. The providers we introduce offer monitoring services to alert you if performance drops, indicating a need for professional inspection.

Will transitioning to solar power take me completely off the grid?

Most residential solar installations remain grid-tied. This means you can draw power from the utility grid at night or when usage exceeds production, and potentially send excess power back to the grid. True off-grid systems require substantial battery storage solutions.
