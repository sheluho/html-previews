Frequently Asked Questions

What does your standard lawn care program include?

Our comprehensive program covers seasonal fertilization, targeted weed control, surface insect management, and regular health assessments to ensure optimal turf vitality year-round.

Do you offer full landscape installation?

Yes, we provide complete landscape installation services. We bring our custom garden designs to life using high-quality plants, soils, and materials suited for your specific environment.

How often should I schedule lawn mowing?

For most residential lawns during the active growing season, we recommend weekly or bi-weekly lawn mowing. This maintains an ideal blade height, promoting deeper roots and a denser, healthier turf.

What kind of tree service do you provide?

Our tree service includes structural pruning, canopy thinning, disease evaluations, and general maintenance designed to keep your trees healthy, safe, and aesthetically pleasing.

Is your garden design customized for each property?

Absolutely. Every garden design is uniquely crafted. We consider your home's architecture, local soil conditions, sunlight exposure, and your personal aesthetic preferences to create a tailored outdoor space.
