Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our event videography process.

What is a cinematic wedding video?

A cinematic wedding video goes beyond traditional documentary-style recording. It uses high-end cameras, creative angles, intentional audio design, and emotional storytelling to create a film-like experience that captures the feeling of your wedding day.

Do you travel for destination wedding videography?

Yes, we frequently travel for destination weddings. Our team is experienced in logistics, equipment transport, and scouting new locations to ensure your wedding film is flawless, no matter where in the world you choose to celebrate.

How unobtrusive is the wedding videographer on the day?

We pride ourselves on a "fly-on-the-wall" approach. Our goal is to capture candid, natural moments without interrupting the flow of your day. We work seamlessly alongside photographers and planners to ensure a smooth experience.

Does your event videography cover rehearsal dinners?

Absolutely. Rehearsal dinners often contain some of the most heartfelt speeches and intimate moments. We offer comprehensive event videography packages that can include multi-day coverage to complete your story.

When will we receive our final wedding film?

Because we meticulously edit and color-grade each frame of your cinematic wedding video, the delivery timeline typically ranges from 12 to 16 weeks, depending on the season and the complexity of your event coverage.
