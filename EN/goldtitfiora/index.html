Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our landscaping and lawn care services.

How often should I schedule lawn mowing?
For optimal turf health, we generally recommend weekly lawn mowing during the peak growing season. This ensures no more than one-third of the grass blade is cut at a time, which promotes deeper roots and a thicker, more resilient lawn.
Do you offer custom garden design?
Yes, our garden design services are highly customized. We assess your property's soil, sunlight exposure, and existing architecture to create a landscape plan that matches your aesthetic preferences and maintenance capabilities.
What is included in your landscape installation process?
Our landscape installation covers everything from site preparation and soil grading to the planting of trees, shrubs, and flowers. We also install edging, mulch, and can coordinate the integration of hardscaping elements to complete the design.
When is the best time for tree service and pruning?
While emergency tree service is available year-round, the best time for structural pruning is typically during the dormant season (late winter). However, light pruning and removal of dead or diseased wood can and should be done at any time to maintain safety and tree health.
Are your landscaping methods safe for pets?
Absolutely. We prioritize the safety of your family and pets. When applying fertilizers or treatments as part of our lawn care programs, we provide clear instructions on how long to keep pets off the grass, and we offer organic and pet-friendly product options upon request.
