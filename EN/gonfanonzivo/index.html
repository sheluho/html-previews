Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical window installation take?

The duration of window installation depends on the number of units being replaced and the complexity of the job. However, professional crews are highly efficient and can often complete a standard whole-house replacement in one to two days, ensuring minimal disruption to your daily life.

What makes energy efficient windows worth the investment?

Energy efficient windows utilize advanced materials like Low-E glass coatings and argon gas fills between panes. These features significantly reduce heat transfer, keeping your home warmer in the winter and cooler in the summer. Over time, this improved insulation can lead to noticeable savings on heating and cooling bills.

Are double hung windows difficult to maintain?

Not at all. In fact, modern double hung windows are designed specifically for easy maintenance. Because both the top and bottom sashes can tilt inward, you can clean both the inside and outside of the glass safely and conveniently from inside your home without needing ladders.

Can replacement windows be ordered in custom sizes?

Yes. Because every home is unique, most high-quality replacement windows are custom-manufactured to fit the exact measurements of your existing window openings. This custom fit ensures a tight, secure seal that maximizes energy efficiency and structural integrity.

What are the main benefits of choosing vinyl windows?

Vinyl windows offer an excellent combination of durability, low maintenance, and affordability. Unlike wood, vinyl frames will not rot, peel, or require repainting over time. They also provide excellent thermal insulation, making them a very practical and long-lasting choice for residential properties.
