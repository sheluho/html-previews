Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries about our planning process.

What exactly does a full-service wedding planning company handle?
A full-service wedding planner manages the entire scope of your event from start to finish. This includes budget development, venue scouting, assembling a team of reputable vendors, creating cohesive design boards, managing contracts, and overseeing the entire weekend's timeline to ensure nothing is overlooked.
Do I really need a day of coordinator if my venue provides a manager?
Yes. A venue manager is responsible for the venue's operations (food, beverage, facilities), while a dedicated day of coordinator works strictly for you. Your coordinator manages your personal timeline, directs the bridal party, coordinates with all external vendors (photographers, florists, bands), and handles any personal emergencies, allowing you to be a guest at your own wedding.
How far in advance should we hire a wedding planner?
Ideally, you should secure your wedding planner 12 to 18 months before your desired date. This allows ample time to secure high-demand venues and top-tier vendors. However, if you are planning on a shorter timeline, professional planners can expedite the process efficiently.
Can you assist with planning a destination wedding?
Absolutely. Destination wedding planning is a specialized service we highly recommend. Coordinating logistics, travel, and international vendors requires dedicated expertise. A professional team ensures that planning from afar is just as seamless as planning locally.
What is the difference between a wedding planner and a wedding coordinator?
A wedding planner works with you from the very beginning, helping to conceptualize the event, hire vendors, and manage the budget. A wedding coordinator (often referred to as a day-of or month-of coordinator) steps in closer to the wedding date to take over the plans you've already made, ensuring they are executed flawlessly on the day itself.
