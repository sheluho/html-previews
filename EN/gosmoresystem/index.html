Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom dining table?

The timeline for a custom dining table typically ranges from 8 to 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of the design and the availability of specific wood species. Every piece of handmade furniture is crafted with precision, ensuring no detail is rushed.

Can I choose the fabric for my custom sofa?

Absolutely. When designing a custom sofa, you have access to a wide array of premium upholstery options, including high-performance fabrics, top-grain leathers, and natural linens. The showroom offers samples to ensure your selection perfectly complements your space.

Do you only make tables and seating?

While custom dining tables and seating are highly requested, bespoke furniture extends to bedroom sets, credenzas, media consoles, built-in cabinetry, and more. If you can envision it, a skilled custom furniture maker can build it.

Where do you source your wood?

Materials are sourced responsibly, often prioritizing local and sustainable timber suppliers in the Pacific Northwest region. Salvaged and live-edge slabs are also available for unique, one-of-a-kind bespoke furniture pieces.

How do I start a bespoke furniture project?

The process begins with a consultation. You can submit an inquiry through the form below to discuss your ideas, dimensions, and material preferences. Following the initial discussion, you will receive a detailed proposal and design sketches for your handmade furniture.
