Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about maintaining your perfect indoor climate.

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

It is highly recommended to have your heating and cooling systems inspected and serviced twice a year. Schedule a check-up for your air conditioner in the spring before the summer heat, and a tune-up for your furnace or heat pump in the fall before winter arrives.

What are the signs I need a new air conditioner?

Key indicators include your AC unit being more than 10-15 years old, requiring frequent or costly repairs, making unusual grinding or squealing noises, or failing to cool your home evenly. An unexpected spike in your energy bills can also signal that your unit is losing efficiency and a new AC installation is warranted.

Do you service both traditional furnaces and heat pumps?

Yes. The professionals we connect you with are fully equipped to handle comprehensive HVAC needs, including the diagnosis, repair, and replacement of both gas/electric furnaces and modern energy-efficient heat pumps.

How long does a typical AC installation take?

Most standard residential air conditioner installations can be completed in a single day (typically 4 to 8 hours). However, if significant ductwork modifications are required or if it's a complex multi-zone system, it may take slightly longer.

Why is my heat pump blowing cool air in the winter?

Heat pumps occasionally go into a normal "defrost cycle" to melt ice off the outdoor coils, which temporarily shifts the system into a cooling mode. However, if it continuously blows cold air, it could indicate low refrigerant, a faulty reversing valve, or a thermostat issue requiring professional service.
