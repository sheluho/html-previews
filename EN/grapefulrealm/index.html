Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to design and install custom closets?

The timeline can vary based on the complexity of your project. Typically, the initial consultation and design phase takes about a week, while manufacturing and installation follow shortly after. We strive to provide accurate timelines during your first meeting.

Can you work with uniquely shaped rooms or small reach-in closets?

Absolutely. Our expertise lies in maximizing challenging spaces. Whether it's an angled ceiling in a walk-in closet or a narrow reach-in closet, our custom designs are built to fit seamlessly into your existing architecture.

Do you offer various finishes and materials for the wardrobe?

Yes, we provide a wide array of premium materials, colors, and hardware options. During your consultation, we will show you samples so you can choose the perfect finish to complement your home's interior design.

What does a closet organizer system include?

A customized closet organizer usually consists of a tailored mix of shelving, hanging rods at various heights, soft-close drawers, shoe racks, and specialized compartments for jewelry or ties, depending entirely on your personal storage needs.

How do I prepare my space before installation day?

We ask that you completely empty the existing closet or space where the new wardrobe will be installed. Removing your personal items ensures our installation team can work safely and efficiently to build your new custom storage solution.
