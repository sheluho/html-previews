Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a standard house cleaning?

A standard clean typically covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and sanitizing bathrooms and kitchens. It ensures all high-traffic areas of your home are spotless and well-maintained on a regular basis.

Do I need to provide the cleaning supplies?

No, the professional house cleaning service teams arrive fully equipped with premium, effective cleaning products and industry-standard tools to handle all tasks efficiently.

How does the recurring cleaning schedule work?

You have the flexibility to choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly intervals based on your specific needs. The schedule is highly adaptable and designed around your lifestyle to ensure consistent upkeep.

What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

Deep cleaning involves more intensive tasks like hand-wiping baseboards, cleaning inside ovens or refrigerators, and scrubbing grout lines. Standard cleaning focuses on general, routine upkeep of visible surfaces.

Is the maid service available for move-out cleaning on weekends?

Yes, scheduling is highly accommodating to ensure your move-out cleaning aligns perfectly with your moving timeline, which often includes availability on weekends. It is recommended to book in advance to secure your preferred slot.
