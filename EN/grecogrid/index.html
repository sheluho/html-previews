Common Questions

Insights into the solar installation journey.

How long does the typical residential solar installation take?

While the actual physical installation of solar panels on your roof usually takes only one to three days, the entire process—including design, permitting, and grid interconnection—can take several weeks to a few months depending on local municipal regulations.

Are solar panels suitable for every type of roof?

Most roofing materials, including asphalt shingles, metal, and tile, are highly suitable for solar installation. However, the condition, age, and shading of your roof are critical factors. A professional site assessment will determine the viability and optimal placement for a photovoltaic array.

What exactly is a photovoltaic system?

A photovoltaic (PV) system utilizes solar panels to capture sunlight and convert it directly into electricity. The system includes the panels themselves, an inverter to convert the generated DC power into usable AC power for your home, and mounting hardware.

Will residential solar eliminate my electric bill entirely?

While solar power significantly reduces your reliance on the grid and lowers monthly costs, most homeowners will still have a minor connection fee from their utility provider. The extent of your savings depends on your energy consumption, system size, and local net metering policies.

Does the system require ongoing maintenance?

Solar panels are incredibly durable and require very little maintenance since they have no moving parts. Occasional cleaning to remove dust or debris, and periodic professional inspections, are usually sufficient to keep your solar power system running at peak efficiency for decades.
