Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about auto glass and windshield repair services.

How long does a full windshield replacement take?

Typically, a professional windshield replacement takes about one to two hours. However, it is highly recommended to wait an additional hour before driving to allow the adhesive to properly cure and ensure maximum safety.

Can my windshield be repaired instead of replaced?

If the damage is smaller than a quarter and is not located directly in the driver's line of sight, a simple windshield repair is often possible. Larger cracks or damage near the edges usually necessitate a complete replacement.

Is chip repair completely invisible?

While chip repair significantly improves the appearance of the auto glass and restores structural integrity, it may not be 100% invisible. A slight blemish might remain, but the primary goal is to prevent the chip from spreading.

Do you handle side and rear car window replacement?

Yes. The professionals we connect you with are fully equipped to handle all types of auto glass, including side windows, rear windshields, and quarter glass.

Will my vehicle's sensors be affected by a new windshield?

Modern vehicles often have Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) mounted on the windshield. A proper windshield replacement service includes recalibration of these sensors to ensure all safety features operate correctly after installation.
