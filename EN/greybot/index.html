Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical bathroom remodel take?

The timeline depends on the scope of the project. A straightforward bathtub replacement might take just a few days, while a comprehensive bathroom renovation involving structural changes and intricate tile installation can take several weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase.

Can you convert my existing tub into a walk-in shower?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most popular services. We remove the old tub, adjust the necessary plumbing, prepare the flooring for proper drainage, and install a custom walk-in shower tailored to your space.

Do I need to purchase my own materials before the remodel?

No, we are a full-service remodeling company. We assist you in selecting high-quality materials—from tiles and grout to vanities and fixtures—and handle the procurement and delivery to ensure everything arrives on time for the installation.

Is your tile installation waterproofed?

Absolutely. Proper waterproofing is critical for any wet area. Before any tile installation begins in a shower or tub surround, we install industry-standard waterproof membranes and backer boards to protect the structural integrity of your home.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical work?

Yes. A complete bathroom remodel often requires rerouting pipes or updating electrical fixtures for new lighting and ventilation. We manage all necessary structural, plumbing, and electrical requirements to ensure a seamless renovation process.
