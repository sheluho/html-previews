Frequently Asked Questions

Insights into the remodeling and design-build process.

What is a turnkey renovation? +

A turnkey renovation means the general contractor handles every single aspect of your project from start to finish. You don't need to hire separate architects, designers, or builders. The firm manages the planning, permitting, materials, and construction, handing you the "keys" to a finished space once completed.

How long does a whole home remodeling project typically take? +

Timelines vary significantly based on the scope of work. Generally, the design and permitting phase can take several months, followed by 4 to 8 months of active construction for a comprehensive whole home remodeling. A precise timeline will be provided by the contractor after the initial consultation.

Do you handle the permits for a home addition? +

Yes, when working with a professional design-build firm, acquiring all necessary city and structural permits is fully managed by their team. They navigate the local building codes to ensure your home addition is fully compliant and legally sound before construction begins.

Why should I hire a design-build general contractor? +

A design-build approach consolidates accountability. Instead of acting as a mediator between your architect and your builder, you work with a unified team. This improves communication, controls costs more effectively, and streamlines the entire home renovation schedule.

What is the first step to start my home renovation? +

The process begins with a preliminary consultation to discuss your vision, budget, and goals. Fill out the inquiry form on our contact section, and you will be connected with the appropriate remodeling experts to evaluate your property and propose a design pathway.
