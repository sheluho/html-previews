Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of spray foam over traditional home insulation?

Spray foam acts as both an insulator and an air sealant. Unlike fiberglass batts, it expands to fill every crack and crevice, preventing conditioned air from escaping and blocking outdoor allergens and moisture from entering your home.

How often should I replace my attic insulation?

Most traditional attic insulation degrades over 15 to 20 years due to moisture, settling, or pest activity. If you notice rising energy bills, drafts, or uneven temperatures across rooms, it is time to have a home insulation contractor inspect and upgrade your space.

What is the difference between vinyl siding and fiber cement siding?

Vinyl siding is highly cost-effective, low-maintenance, and comes in a vast array of colors. Fiber cement siding is thicker, incredibly durable, fire-resistant, and closely mimics the texture of natural wood. Both are excellent choices for modernizing your house siding.

Can you install house siding over existing materials?

In some cases, new house siding can be installed over existing wood or T1-11 siding if the underlying structure is sound and free of rot. However, for best longevity and to inspect the wall cavity for structural issues or missing home insulation, we often recommend a full tear-off.

Do you provide estimates for home insulation and siding contractor services?

Yes. We offer comprehensive on-site evaluations to assess your specific property needs. Whether you need isolated attic insulation, a full-home spray foam encapsulation, or a complete exterior facelift with new vinyl siding, we provide clear, upfront pricing.
