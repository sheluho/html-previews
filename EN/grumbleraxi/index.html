Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries about our landscaping services.

How often should I schedule lawn mowing?

For optimal grass health, we recommend weekly or bi-weekly lawn mowing during the growing season. This regular schedule helps maintain an even height, deters weeds, and promotes a denser, more resilient turf.

Do you offer custom garden design?

Absolutely. Our landscape installation process begins with a tailored garden design phase. We consider your property's soil, sunlight, and aesthetic preferences to curate a planting layout that thrives in your specific environment.

What does your tree service include?

Our comprehensive tree service covers everything from regular pruning and canopy shaping to the safe removal of dead or hazardous trees. We focus on maintaining the health of your existing trees while ensuring the safety of your property.

Are your landscaping services available for commercial properties?

Yes, we provide extensive lawn care and landscape installation services for both residential homes and commercial business locations. We understand the importance of a well-maintained exterior for making a positive first impression on your clients.

When is the best time for landscape installation?

Spring and early fall are generally the best times for landscape installation, as the moderate temperatures put less stress on new plants, allowing roots to establish properly before extreme summer heat or winter freezes. However, we can perform certain installations year-round depending on the project scope.
