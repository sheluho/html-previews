Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you cater?

We connect clients with professionals who handle a wide array of gatherings. This includes comprehensive wedding catering, large-scale corporate catering, and intimate private event catering.

Do you offer both plated dinner and buffet catering options?

Yes, the culinary teams offer flexible service styles. You can choose a formal, multi-course plated dinner for an elegant atmosphere, or a diverse buffet catering setup to offer guests a wider variety of choices.

Can menus be customized for dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Custom event catering menus can be designed to accommodate vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, and other specific dietary requirements without compromising on taste or presentation.

How far in advance should we book our event catering?

For wedding catering, it is highly recommended to secure your date 6 to 12 months in advance. For corporate catering and private parties, reaching out 4 to 8 weeks prior is generally sufficient, though earlier is always better.

Are waitstaff and rentals included in the packages?

Full-service packages typically include professional waitstaff, basic linens, and essential tableware. Specific rental needs can be discussed during your consultation to ensure your event catering is fully supported.
