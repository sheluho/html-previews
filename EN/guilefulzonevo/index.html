Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our kitchen remodeling process.

What is included in a turnkey kitchen remodeling project?

A turnkey kitchen remodel means we handle the entire project from start to finish. As a full-service kitchen renovation company, this includes initial design, material sourcing, demolition, structural changes, plumbing, electrical work, cabinet installation, and placing your new countertops. You won't need to hire secondary contractors.

Do you build custom kitchen cabinets or use pre-fabricated ones?

We specialize in custom kitchen cabinetry tailored to your exact floor plan and storage needs. While we can source semi-custom options to fit specific budgets, our expertise lies in crafting bespoke cabinets that maximize your space and elevate your home's aesthetic.

What types of countertops do you offer?

We offer a wide selection of premium countertops, including natural granite, luxurious marble, and highly durable engineered quartz. During the design phase, our team will help you select the best material that fits both your visual preferences and lifestyle demands.

How long does a full-service kitchen renovation usually take?

The timeline for kitchen remodeling varies based on the project's scope, structural changes, and material lead times. Generally, a comprehensive renovation can take anywhere from 4 to 8 weeks once construction begins. We provide a detailed schedule upfront so you know exactly what to expect.

Can I keep my existing layout and just update cabinets and countertops?

Absolutely. If you are happy with your current footprint, we can perform a "pull-and-replace" service. We will carefully remove the old materials and install your new custom cabinets and fresh countertops without altering the underlying plumbing or electrical architecture, saving both time and cost.
