Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our wedding planning services.

What is the difference between a full-service wedding planner and a day of coordinator?

A full-service wedding planner is involved from the very beginning, helping with budget, design, and hiring all vendors. A day of coordinator (or month-of coordinator) steps in closer to the wedding date to take over the plans you have already made, ensuring they are executed smoothly on the actual day.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes, we specialize in destination wedding planning. We are equipped to travel and handle all remote logistics, ensuring that your celebration is just as seamless abroad as it would be close to home.

When should we hire a wedding coordinator?

We recommend securing your wedding planning team as soon as you are engaged, ideally 12-18 months in advance. However, if you are looking specifically for a day of coordinator, reaching out 6-8 months prior to your date is advisable to ensure availability.

Will you work with vendors we have already chosen?

Absolutely. Whether you need partial planning or full coordination, we are happy to collaborate with the vendors you have already selected and can provide recommendations for any remaining categories you need to fill.

How does the consultation process work?

Our process begins with an initial inquiry form followed by a complimentary consultation call. This allows us to understand your vision, discuss your specific needs, and determine which of our wedding planning services will best support your big day.
