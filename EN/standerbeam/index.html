Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about auto detailing and paint protection.

What is a ceramic coating and why do I need it?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied to the exterior of a vehicle. It chemically bonds with the factory paint, creating a protective layer that is far more durable than traditional wax. It protects against UV rays, chemical stains, and minor scratches while providing a high-gloss finish that makes washing your car significantly easier.

How long does interior detailing take?

A comprehensive interior detailing usually takes between 3 to 5 hours, depending on the size and condition of the vehicle. This process includes deep vacuuming, steam cleaning, stain removal, and conditioning of all surfaces to ensure a thorough revitalization of the cabin.

Do you offer paint correction before applying a coating?

Yes, paint correction is a crucial step before applying any long-term paint protection. We meticulously polish the clear coat to remove swirl marks, oxidation, and light scratches. This ensures the surface is perfectly smooth and flawless before the ceramic coating locks that finish in place.

How often should I have my car professionally detailed?

We recommend a full car detailing (interior and exterior) every 4 to 6 months to maintain its condition. However, if your vehicle is coated with a premium ceramic layer, routine maintenance washes every few weeks are usually sufficient to keep it looking its best.

What makes your auto detailing service different?

We prioritize quality over speed, utilizing premium products and advanced techniques recommended by top providers like ZJ Detailing. Our rigorous process ensures lasting protection and a flawless finish that generic, high-volume car washes simply cannot match.
