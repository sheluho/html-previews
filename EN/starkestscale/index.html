Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our promoted professional house cleaning services.

What is typically included in standard recurring cleaning?

Standard recurring maid service generally covers dusting of accessible surfaces, vacuuming carpets and rugs, sweeping and mopping hard floors, and thorough cleaning of bathrooms and kitchens. It is designed to maintain a high baseline of cleanliness in your home on a regular schedule.

Do I need to be present during the maid service?

It is entirely up to your preference. Many clients provide secure access to their homes so the professionals can work while the home is empty. Others prefer to be present. The service providers are fully vetted professionals accustomed to working independently.

What distinguishes standard cleaning from deep cleaning?

While standard cleaning focuses on regular upkeep, deep cleaning is an intensive service. It targets areas not typically covered in a routine visit, such as scrubbing baseboards, cleaning inside appliances, detailed dusting of high or hard-to-reach areas, and intensive grout cleaning.

Are cleaning supplies and equipment provided?

Yes, the professionals arriving at your home are fully equipped with all necessary, high-quality cleaning supplies and equipment required to perform a comprehensive house cleaning. If you have specific preferred products, you may discuss this prior to your service.

How can I schedule a move-out cleaning?

Scheduling is simple. You can use the contact form on this page to request a consultation. We will forward your details so you can receive an accurate estimate tailored to the size of your property and the specific requirements of your move-out cleaning.
