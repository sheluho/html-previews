Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about commissioning custom furniture.

What is the typical process for ordering custom furniture?
The process generally begins with a consultation at a custom furniture maker showroom or via phone. You will discuss your vision, dimensions, and material preferences. The maker then provides a design sketch and a quote. Once approved, construction begins, culminating in the delivery of your bespoke furniture.
How long does it take to build a custom dining table?
Lead times vary depending on the complexity of the design and the current schedule of the artisan. Typically, a handcrafted custom dining table takes between 8 to 14 weeks from the final design approval to completion. High-quality handmade furniture requires time for proper wood acclimation, meticulous joinery, and multi-step finishing.
Can I choose the specific fabric for a custom sofa?
Absolutely. One of the main benefits of a custom sofa is the ability to select from hundreds of upholstery options. Showrooms typically offer a wide range of materials, including performance fabrics, top-grain leathers, velvets, and natural linens, ensuring the final piece matches your lifestyle and decor perfectly.
Is handmade furniture more durable than mass-produced pieces?
Yes, significantly. Handmade furniture utilizes traditional joinery techniques (like mortise and tenon or dovetail joints) and solid, high-grade materials rather than veneers over particleboard. This meticulous construction process ensures that bespoke furniture can withstand daily use and often lasts for generations as heirloom pieces.
Do you offer design consultations at the showroom?
Yes, visiting a custom furniture maker showroom is highly recommended. During a showroom consultation, you can view past work, examine material samples, and discuss your spatial requirements with experts who specialize in translating concepts into functional, beautiful bespoke furniture.
