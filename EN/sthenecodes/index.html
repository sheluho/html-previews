Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main benefit of spray foam insulation?

Spray foam is one of the most effective home insulation materials available. It expands to fill gaps and cracks, creating an airtight seal that prevents drafts, blocks moisture, and significantly reduces heating and cooling costs compared to traditional materials.

Why should I consider fiber cement siding over vinyl siding?

While vinyl siding is excellent for affordability and low maintenance, fiber cement siding offers superior durability. It is highly resistant to fire, rot, and termites, and can perfectly mimic the look of natural wood siding for a more premium exterior appearance.

Does upgrading attic insulation really save money?

Yes. Heat rises, meaning poorly insulated attics are a primary source of energy loss in a home. By adding proper attic insulation, you keep heated air inside during winter and block radiant heat during the summer, easing the load on your HVAC system and lowering utility bills.

How do I know if it's time to replace my house siding?

Signs that you need new house siding include visible cracks, severe fading, warping, or peeling paint inside your home (which indicates moisture is penetrating the exterior). An evaluation from a siding contractor can definitively tell you if repair or replacement is best.
