Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a moving company?

We recommend booking your movers at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance, especially if you plan to move during peak seasons like summer or at the end of the month. Early booking ensures availability and gives you ample time to prepare.

Do you provide packing supplies with your packing services?

Yes. When you select our comprehensive packing services, the professional teams arrive fully equipped with durable boxes, bubble wrap, packing paper, and specialized containers for wardrobes and fragile items. You do not need to supply anything.

What is the difference between local movers and long distance movers?

Local moves typically occur within a 50-mile radius and are usually billed at an hourly rate. Long distance moves cross state lines or cover significant mileage; these are generally priced based on the total weight of your inventory and the distance traveled.

Are my belongings insured during the move?

Basic liability coverage is typically included with professional moving services, which covers items at a set rate per pound. For higher-value items, we strongly recommend discussing full-value protection options with the provider before the move begins.

How can I prepare my home before the movers arrive?

To ensure a smooth process, clear all walkways of clutter and disconnect major appliances. If you are not utilizing our packing services, ensure all boxes are securely taped and clearly labeled by room. Keep important personal documents and valuables with you.
