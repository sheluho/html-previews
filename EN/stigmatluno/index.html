Common Questions

Why should I choose fiberglass doors for my entryway?

Fiberglass doors are highly recommended for front door replacement because they offer the aesthetic appeal of real wood without the maintenance. They resist warping, rotting, and denting, while providing excellent insulation to improve your home's energy efficiency.

How long does a typical door installation take?

The duration varies based on the scope. A standard interior door swap might take an hour or two per door, while a full front door replacement involving frame modifications can take a half to a full day. The professionals we connect you with will provide a specific timeline for your project.

Do you handle interior doors as well as exterior entries?

Yes, our promotional network covers both. Whether you need a secure, weather-resistant front door or are looking to upgrade all the interior doors in your home for better soundproofing and style, the service providers can accommodate your needs.

Will new interior doors help with noise reduction?

Absolutely. Replacing hollow-core builder-grade doors with solid core interior doors significantly dampens sound transmission between rooms, creating a quieter, more private environment in bedrooms and home offices.

Does Stigmat Luno perform the installations directly?

No. Stigmat Luno is an independent promotional operator. We introduce you to specialized door installation experts, such as Interior Door and Closet, who handle the physical measurements, custom ordering, and final installation of your doors.
