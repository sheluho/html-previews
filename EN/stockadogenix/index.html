Common Inquiries

Guidance on selecting the perfect window treatments.

How do I choose between blinds and shades?

The choice depends on your functional needs and aesthetic preferences. Blinds feature slats that can be tilted to adjust light precisely, while shades are made of continuous fabric that rolls or folds up. Shades generally offer softer textures, whereas blinds provide structural lines and excellent directional light control.

Are plantation shutters energy efficient?

Yes, plantation shutters are among the most energy-efficient window treatments available. Because they are custom-fitted and mounted tightly to the window frame, they create an excellent insulating barrier that helps regulate indoor temperatures, potentially reducing heating and cooling costs.

Can roller shades provide complete darkness?

Absolutely. Roller shades are available in various opacity levels, including blackout fabrics. For bedrooms or media rooms requiring maximum darkness, we recommend blackout roller shades paired with side channels to prevent light from seeping in around the edges.

Do you design custom drapes for unusually shaped windows?

Yes, custom drapes are an excellent solution for large, arched, or unconventionally shaped windows. Because they are made to order, we can design tracking systems and fabric panels that elegantly frame any architectural feature without compromising on style or functionality.

How should I prepare for a design consultation?

Simply think about your primary goals for each room—whether it's privacy, light filtering, energy efficiency, or pure aesthetics. Gathering inspiration photos can also be helpful. During the consultation, we will assess your space, discuss your needs, and present tailored options from our collections.
