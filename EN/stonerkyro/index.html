Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we secure a wedding photographer?

It is generally recommended to book your wedding photographer 9 to 12 months in advance, especially if you are planning a ceremony during the peak wedding season. Early booking ensures that your preferred dates are secured.

Do you offer engagement photos as part of the experience?

Yes, engaging a photographer for your engagement photos is highly encouraged. It serves as a wonderful opportunity to build rapport with the professional behind the camera and results in beautiful imagery for your save-the-dates.

Can we request specific bridal portraits?

Absolutely. You can collaborate directly with the wedding photography team to create a personalized shot list, ensuring that all desired bridal portraits, family groupings, and special details are captured exactly as you envision them.

Is destination wedding photography supported?

Yes, we promote studios equipped to handle destination wedding photography. These professionals are well-versed in travel logistics and adept at utilizing unique landscapes to enhance your wedding gallery.

How long does it take to receive the final wedding photography gallery?

Delivery times vary depending on the specific studio and the season. However, most couples can expect to receive their fully edited, high-resolution wedding photography gallery within 6 to 8 weeks after the event.
