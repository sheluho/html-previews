Common Questions

Insights to help you plan your home upgrade.

What are the main benefits of replacement windows?

Beyond an immediate aesthetic upgrade, replacement windows significantly improve insulation. This translates to lower heating and cooling costs, reduced exterior noise infiltration, and increased overall property value. Modern frames also require far less maintenance than older wood models.

How do I know if I need energy efficient windows?

If you experience drafts near your frames, notice condensation between glass panes, have difficulty opening or closing sashes, or see unexplained spikes in your utility bills, it is likely time to consider an upgrade. Energy efficient windows address all these issues with advanced sealing and glazing technologies.

Why are vinyl windows so popular?

Vinyl windows offer an exceptional balance of affordability, durability, and energy performance. The material does not rot, flake, or peel, meaning they never need to be painted. They also feature multi-chambered frames that trap air, providing excellent thermal barriers.

What exactly are double hung windows?

Double hung windows feature two operable sashes (the framed glass panels) that slide vertically up and down within the frame. This allows you to open either the top, the bottom, or both simultaneously, promoting superior air circulation. Many modern designs also tilt inward for easy cleaning from inside the home.

How long does a typical window installation take?

A professional window installation team can typically replace 10 to 15 windows in a single day. The exact timeline depends on the complexity of the job, the condition of the existing frames, and the type of installation (insert vs. full-frame tear-out). The focus is always on precision and minimizing disruption to your daily life.
