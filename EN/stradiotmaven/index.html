Frequently Asked Questions

Why is professional tire storage better than keeping them in my garage?

Garages experience extreme temperature fluctuations and humidity, which can cause rubber compounds to degrade and dry rot over time. Professional tire storage facilities are climate-controlled, keeping your tires in an optimal environment to extend their usable life. Plus, it frees up valuable space in your home.

How often should I schedule a tire rotation?

Generally, a tire rotation is recommended every 5,000 to 7,500 miles, or every time you get an oil change. Regular rotation ensures that your tires wear evenly across all four wheels, which maximizes their lifespan and maintains optimal traction and handling.

Do I need a wheel alignment when getting a tire replacement?

Yes, it is highly recommended. Getting a wheel alignment with your tire replacement ensures that your new tires meet the road at the correct angle. If your alignment is off, your new tires can suffer from rapid, uneven wear, reducing their lifespan and potentially compromising your vehicle's safety.

What is included in the tire installation service?

A standard tire installation includes removing the old tires, mounting the new tires onto your wheels, replacing the valve stems (if applicable), and computer balancing the wheel and tire assembly to prevent vibrations. The wheels are then bolted back onto your vehicle and torqued to manufacturer specifications.

How do I know when it's time for new tires?

You should consider a tire replacement if the tread depth is below 2/32 of an inch, if you notice uneven wear patterns, visible cracks, bulges, or if the tires are more than six years old, regardless of tread wear. Our technicians can perform a thorough inspection to advise you on their condition.
