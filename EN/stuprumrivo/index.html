Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need new house siding?

Signs that it is time to replace your house siding include visible cracks, severe fading, peeling paint, or instances of dry rot. If your home requires frequent painting or if you notice higher heating and cooling bills, upgrading to modern vinyl siding or fiber cement siding can resolve these issues permanently.

What are the benefits of spray foam over traditional insulation?

Spray foam is highly superior because it expands to fill gaps and cracks that traditional fiberglass batts might miss. This creates an airtight seal, preventing drafts, minimizing moisture entry, and providing exceptional thermal resistance for ultimate home insulation.

Is fiber cement siding better than vinyl siding?

Both materials are excellent, but they serve different needs. Fiber cement siding is generally thicker, highly fire-resistant, and can closely mimic the texture of real wood, but it typically costs more. Vinyl siding is incredibly cost-effective, low-maintenance, and never requires repainting. Your contractor can help you decide based on your budget and aesthetic goals.

How much can attic insulation save me on energy bills?

Because heat naturally rises, a poorly insulated attic is a major source of energy loss. Upgrading your attic insulation can reduce your heating and cooling costs by 10% to 50%, depending on your home's current state and the climate in your region.

How long does a typical home insulation upgrade take?

Most standard home insulation projects, such as adding blown-in or spray foam attic insulation, can be completed in just one to two days. Larger whole-house retrofits may take slightly longer, but contractors work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily life.
