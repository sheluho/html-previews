Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the carpet take to dry?
Drying times vary depending on airflow, humidity, and the specific carpet type. However, because the steam cleaning process is highly efficient at extraction, carpets typically dry within a few hours, rather than days, allowing you to return to normal life quickly.
Is your stain removal process safe for pets?
The methods promoted focus on eliminating the use of harsh, toxic chemicals. The primary cleaning agent leaves zero sticky residue behind, meaning your floors are safe for pets and children to play on immediately after drying.
What types of upholstery can be cleaned?
Professional upholstery cleaning covers a wide range of fabrics including synthetic blends, cotton, microfiber, and more delicate materials. Technicians assess the fabric type before applying any cleaning solution to ensure the safest and most effective results.
How often should I schedule carpet cleaning?
For most households, scheduling a professional deep clean every 12 to 18 months is recommended. Homes with indoor pets, high traffic, or allergy sufferers may benefit from professional cleaning every 6 to 9 months to maintain optimal indoor air quality.
Can you clean delicate area rugs?
Yes, specialized rug cleaning is available. Area rugs, especially Oriental or wool rugs, require a different approach than wall-to-wall carpeting. The process is adjusted to gently yet thoroughly clean delicate fibers without causing shrinkage or color bleeding.
