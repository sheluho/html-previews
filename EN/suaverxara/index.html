Common Questions

Insights into the coordination process.

What exactly does a full-service wedding planner do?

A full-service planner manages the entire scope of your event. This includes developing a realistic budget, sourcing and negotiating with appropriate vendors, designing the visual aesthetic, managing guest accommodations, and overseeing all logistics leading up to and including the wedding day itself.

Do I really need a day of coordinator if my venue has a manager?

Yes. A venue manager is responsible for the venue's operations (food, building maintenance, staff). A day of coordinator works exclusively for you, ensuring your personal timeline is met, external vendors arrive on time, the bridal party is organized, and all your personal items and decor are set up correctly.

When should we hire a wedding planner?

Ideally, you should hire a full-service planner right after you get engaged, before selecting a venue or setting a date. If you are only looking for a wedding coordinator for month-of management, booking them 6 to 8 months in advance is recommended to secure their availability.

Can you help plan a destination wedding outside of Texas?

Absolutely. The professionals we highlight have extensive experience managing destination events. They handle the unique challenges of remote planning, including timezone differences, local legal requirements for marriage, and coordinating multi-day activities for traveling guests.

How are budgets managed during the planning process?

A professional planner will start by helping you establish a realistic budget based on your priorities and local market rates. They track all expenditures, remind you when payments are due, and actively negotiate with vendors to ensure you receive the best value without compromising on quality.
