Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main benefits of spray foam over traditional insulation?
Spray foam provides a continuous, airtight barrier that traditional fiberglass batts simply cannot match. It expands to fill every crack and crevice, drastically reducing air leaks, blocking moisture accumulation, and providing a higher R-value per inch, which leads to significant energy savings.
How do I know if my house needs new attic insulation?
Common signs include unusually high heating and cooling bills, drafts in the upper levels of your home, difficulty maintaining a consistent temperature, or visible ice dams on your roof during winter. A visual inspection revealing compressed or thin existing insulation is also a strong indicator.
What is the difference between vinyl siding and fiber cement siding?
Vinyl siding is highly cost-effective, extremely low maintenance, and comes in a wide variety of colors. Fiber cement siding is thicker, offers exceptional durability against harsh weather and fire, and can closely mimic the texture of real wood or masonry, though it typically requires a higher initial investment.
Does proper home insulation actually lower energy bills?
Yes, absolutely. Heating and cooling account for the majority of a home's energy usage. By creating a thermal envelope with proper home insulation, your HVAC system doesn't have to work as hard to maintain your desired temperature, which directly translates to lower monthly utility costs.
How long does a typical house siding replacement take?
The timeline varies based on the size of the home and the type of materials used. Generally, an average-sized home takes about one to two weeks to complete, including the removal of old siding, preparation of the exterior walls, and the precise installation of the new panels.
